## Verification (tests, plus F1.md)

1. **The frequency mapping.**
   - For σ ∈ {2, 8, 24, 96}, the variance kept per octave by `DCTBlur` on the IXI `train` split
     must equal the prediction $\sum d^2P/\sum P$ within 1e-6 (relative).
   - The half-power frequency $c_{1/2}=25.4/\sigma$ must match a numeric root to 1%.
   - $c = 43.2/\sigma$ is the mode length-scale; state that in the caption.
2. **The level counts per σ-octave** of both schedules equal `docs/RESULTS/data_profile.md` §5:
   `log_W2` 27/26/26/26/27/26/26/16 and `ixi_W8` 24/37/46/43/32/18/0/0.
3. **The annotated numbers** equal their sources:
   - octave shares;
   - inherited shares 0.3% and 8.5%;
   - KID −63%;
   - seed-NN 6% → 72%.
4. **Visual check.** View the PNG and iterate until everything is legible at 5.5 in: no overlapping
   labels, arrows land on their frequency, thumbnails are not distorted. In the log, list what you
   changed after looking.
5. **Byte stability.** Two consecutive runs produce identical PDF and PNG bytes; check the sha256.
