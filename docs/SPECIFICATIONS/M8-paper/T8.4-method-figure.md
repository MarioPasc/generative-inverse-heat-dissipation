**Purpose.** The key figure that explains IHDM in the frequency language the paper uses. Mario's
brief (2026-10-07), condensed:

- **A top box** with rounded corners: an MRI image = Σ w·mode, with the modes shown as images, the
  weights as LaTeX $w_{i,j}$, and the modes of one octave sharing a colour.
- **A middle panel** of forward and backward rows: each forward step removes an octave (bracket
  "−(modes…)" above the arrow, the transition probability below); each backward step restores it
  ("+(modes…)").
- **A thin vertical panel** on the right, from the top box down to the bottom of the
  forward/backward panel: a spectrum like the proposal's, plus what the schedule does (inter-octave
  variance).
- MRI images throughout.

## Scientific content that must be exact (from `learning/01-ihdm-method.md` and Rissanen et al., ICLR 2023, §3; verify against them)

1. **The DCT decomposition.**
   - $\mathbf x=\sum_{i,j}w_{i,j}\,\varphi_{i,j}$, with $\varphi_{i,j}$ the **orthonormal 2-D DCT-II
     basis images** (192 × 192) and $w_{i,j}$ the DCT coefficients of $\mathbf x$.
   - A mode $(i,j)$ has radial index $n=\sqrt{i^2+j^2}$ and carries $c=n/2$ **cycles per image**
     (`05-metrics.md` §1).
   - **An octave** is the band $[c, 2c)$: the project's 8 octaves are 0.5–1, 1–2, …, 32–64,
     64–96 c/img (the last one closed at 96).
   - In the $(i,j)$ index plane an octave is a quarter-annulus.
   - The DC mode $(0,0)$ is the image mean and belongs to no octave; draw it grey.
2. **The forward process (heat dissipation).**
   - Per mode, $\tilde u_{i,j}(t)=e^{-\lambda_{i,j}t}\,\tilde u_{i,j}(0)$, with
     $\lambda_{i,j}=(\pi i/W)^2+(\pi j/W)^2$ and $t=\sigma_B^2/2$ (the released `DCTBlur`).
   - IHDM's forward process is **not** a step-to-step Markov chain:
     $q(\mathbf u_k\mid\mathbf u_0)=\mathcal N(\mathbf u_k;\,A_k\mathbf u_0,\,\sigma^2 I)$, i.e. the
     data blurred to level $k$ plus noise σ = 0.01.
   - **Label the forward arrows with $q(\mathbf u_k\mid\mathbf u_0)$**, not
     $q(\mathbf u_k\mid\mathbf u_{k-1})$.
3. **The reverse process.** $p_\theta(\mathbf u_{k-1}\mid\mathbf u_k)=\mathcal N(\mu_\theta(\mathbf u_k,k),\,\delta^2 I)$,
   with δ = 1.25σ. Sampling starts from the **prior**: a training image blurred to
   $\sigma_{B,\max}$, plus noise. Label the reverse arrows with
   $p_\theta(\mathbf u_{k-1}\mid\mathbf u_k)$.
4. **"One step = one octave" is a macro-step. Say so in the caption.**
   - The chain has K = 200 levels, so a single level does not remove a whole octave.
   - A blur $\sigma_B$ multiplies a mode at $c$ c/img by $d=\exp(-\sigma_B^2/\sigma_n^2)$, with
     $\sigma_n\approx 43.2/c$ px.
   - The figure therefore shows the states at $\sigma_b = 43.2/c_b$ for each octave's lower edge
     $c_b$. At that state:
     - modes at $c_b$ keep $d=e^{-1}$;
     - modes at $c \ge 2c_b$ keep $d \le e^{-4} \approx 0.018$.
   - Each arrow therefore spans "the levels that act on that octave". **Print those level counts**:
     default (log) and matched (IXI-matched), per frequency octave, as computed in `paper_f1.py`:
     - log_W2: 31/26/26/26/27/26/26/12;
     - ixi_W8: 0/5/30/38/47/42/29/9 (octaves 0.5–1 → 64–96).
5. **What the schedule does** (in the vertical panel, in about 2 lines):
   - the schedule places the K levels;
   - log spacing gives every octave the same number of levels, which is right when every octave
     carries the same between-image variance (a 1/f² spectrum);
   - the IXI-matched spacing makes each level remove the same between-image variance, so octaves
     that carry more variance get more levels.

   Show IXI's variance share per octave (`data_profile/ixi.npz` `octave_shares_train`: 4.1 / 11.3 /
   11.0 / 16.8 / **29.8** / 18.3 / 7.8 / 1.0%) beside the two level counts.
6. **Where the two priors start.**
   - The default prior (σ = 96 px, W/2) sits beyond the coarsest octave.
   - The matched prior (σ = 24 px, W/8) keeps the octaves below about 1.8 c/img.
   - Mark both on the forward row ("default prior", "matched prior": the chain starts here).

  0 / 1 / 2;

**Tests:**

1. **Reconstruction:** $\sum w_{i,j}\varphi_{i,j}$ equals the image (allclose, 1e-10); the basis is
   orthonormal on a small grid.
2. **The octave masks:** they partition the non-DC modes with $c \le 96$; the counts per octave are
   printed in `FM.md`.
3. **The macro-steps:** at each $\sigma_b$, the mean $d$ of modes at $c_b$ is $e^{-1}$ (within the
   radial binning), and every mode with $c \ge 2c_b$ has $d \le e^{-4}$.
4. **The level counts** per frequency octave equal those of `paper_f1` (import its function; do not
   copy it).
5. **Byte stability**, and **CLI exit codes** 2 (missing input) and 1 (analysis error).
