## Panel (a): samples (common random numbers across arms)

- **The runs** are `ixi_A0_s1`, `ixi_A2_s1`, `ixi_A1_s1` and `ixi_A3_s1`, from the SanDisk tars
  `…/evaluation/eval_2488269/<run>_amp-fp16.tar`.
- **The members** are `<run>/samples_amp-fp16/060000/{final,heldout}/{samples.npy, seed_idx.npy, seeds.npy, request.json}`.
  - `final/samples.npy` is uint8 `[2000, 192, 192]`, with one training seed per sample.
  - `heldout/samples.npy` is uint8 `[40·50, 192, 192]`, seed-major.
- **Assert CRN.** The four runs must have identical `final/seed_idx.npy` and
  `heldout/seed_idx.npy`, and identical `rng_seed` (0 for the final set, 2026 for the held-out set)
  and `batch_size` (32).
- **The rows** follow a pre-declared rule:
  - final-set positions 0 and 1 (expected images 3311 and 2488);
  - held-out seed position 0, sample 0 (expected image 5).
  - *Skip rule:* a seed image whose fraction of pixels above 0.1 is below 10% (a near-empty top or
    bottom slice) is replaced by the next position. Log every skip and print it in `F2.md`.
- **The columns:**
  - seed;
  - default-prior state $u(96)$, then default, then +spacing;
  - matched-prior state $u(24)$, then +prior, then matched.
  - The prior states are noise-free, from `model_code/utils.py::DCTBlur`.
  - Group the headers "default prior (W/2)" and "matched prior (W/8)".

## T1: the main table

- **The source:** `docs/RESULTS/tables/tables.json`, or `index.csv` through
  `ihdm.stats.cell_table`. The production values (precision against the full 800-image `ref`).
- **Rows:** IXI default, +spacing, +prior, matched; OASIS-1 default, matched.
- **Columns:**
  - n;
  - KID ↓ (pre-registered);
  - LSD ↓ (final 2k set, pre-registered);
  - precision ↑;
  - recall ↑;
  - seed-NN ↓;
  - $D_{pix}$ ↑ (×10³).
- **Format:** the mean, with the per-seed [min, max] on a second line or in small type.
- **The caption** notes:
  - precision here is against the full reference, while F2(b) uses R⁻;
  - with 2–3 seeds an interval is [min, max];
  - "all seeds agree in sign" is not significance.
