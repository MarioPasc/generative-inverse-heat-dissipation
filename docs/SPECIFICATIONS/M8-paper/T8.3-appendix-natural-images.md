## The message

- **The same configurations were trained on LSUN Churches and Bedrooms.**
- **The training objective plateaued**, but at our budget the samples do not reach usable quality.
  The cause is expected (see `00-framing.md` §2), and the tone is "out of reach at this budget",
  never "failure".
- **The caption must state** that the IHDM loss is dominated by irreducible training noise (about
  98% of each level's regression target, `learning/03` §6), so its plateau does not mean the
  samples converged. The thumbnails carry that evidence.

## Figure A1 (5.5 × ≤ 2.0 in)

**Panel (a): the training loss against iteration.**

- **The runs:** `lsun_church_A0_s{1,2,3}` and `lsun_bedroom_A0_s{1,2}` (the default
  configuration).
- **The data:** `$IHDM_DATA_ROOT/_results/runs/<run>/metrics.canonical.jsonl`, read exactly as
  fig. 7 reads it: `ihdm/analysis/figures.py`, `canonical_records`.
- **Plotting:**
  - a running mean over 1,000 iterations;
  - seed means thick and runs thin, in two greys or two line styles (one per dataset);
  - a log y axis.
- **A second x axis on top** gives epochs: 3,200 training images / batch 16 = 200 iterations per
  epoch, so 60k iterations = 300 epochs.
- **Dots mark the checkpoints** of panel (b): 5k, 15k, 30k, 45k and 60k.
- **The 40k → 60k extension** (resume at 40,001) is lightly shaded, as in fig. 7.

**Panel (b): the samples across training.**

- **The rows:** Churches and Bedrooms, run seed 1 (A0).
- **The columns:** the seed image, then its sample at 5k, 15k, 30k, 45k and 60k.
- **The seed** is the first index of the dataset's frozen `eval_seeds_500.npy`.
- **The source:** the evaluation LSD sets in the SanDisk tars,
  `…/evaluation/eval_2488269/<run>_amp-fp16.tar` →
  `<run>/samples_amp-fp16/<step>/lsd/{samples.npy, seed_idx.npy, seeds.npy, request.json}`.
  `samples.npy` is uint8 `[500, 192, 192]`; extract only these members.
- **Assert common random numbers** across the five checkpoints: identical `seed_idx.npy`, the same
  `rng_seed` and the same `batch_size` in every `request.json`.
