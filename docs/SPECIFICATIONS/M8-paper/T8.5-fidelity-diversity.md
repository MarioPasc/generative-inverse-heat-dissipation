**Purpose.** The figure for the professor's rule 3 (harness H-P3: "measures that account for both
fidelity and diversity"). F2 already shows realism against copying (F2b) and the per-octave error
(F2c), and T1 lists the endpoints. F3 adds the two things the paper still cannot show:

- **(a)** per-sample fidelity and population diversity move *together* when generation is seeded from
  2,000 training images, and apart when it is seeded from 40 unseen subjects. W/8's population
  diversity is inherited from the seed set (claim C5);
- **(b)** within-seed diversity: what an ideal sampler would regenerate against what the models
  regenerate. Every model under-disperses (C6), and W/8 trades within-seed variety (−23%, C4) for
  population spread inherited from the seeds (C3).

## Scientific content that must be exact

1. **The two sample sets** (`results_discussion.md` §4.1, `heldout_fidelity/README.md`):
   - **F**: 2,000 *training*-seeded samples, one per seed image;
   - **H**: 40 *unseen* subjects (slice 5 of each) × 50 samples per seed.
   - **The reference** for both is R⁻: the `ref` split without the 40 seed subjects (400 MRI images).
     Inception features, k = 5 (Kynkäänniemi et al., NeurIPS 2019).
2. **Panel (a) numbers.** `runs.<id>.metrics.{F_rminus,H_rminus}.{precision,recall}` of
   `docs/RESULTS/heldout_fidelity/heldout_fidelity.json` (IXI A0, A1, A3; OASIS-1 A0, A3) and
   `docs/RESULTS/paper/f2_a2_heldout/heldout_fidelity.json` (IXI A2). The A0 runs appear in both
   files and must be identical (reuse `paper_f2.merge_heldout`). Seed means the build must reproduce
   (±0.0005):

   | dataset | configuration | n | F precision | F recall | H precision | H recall |
   |---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | IXI | default (A0) | 3 | 0.655 | 0.343 | 0.661 | 0.238 |
   | IXI | +spacing (A2) | 2 | 0.668 | 0.395 | 0.683 | 0.237 |
   | IXI | +prior (A1) | 2 | 0.700 | 0.437 | 0.715 | 0.094 |
   | IXI | matched (A3) | 3 | 0.757 | 0.463 | 0.759 | 0.082 |
   | OASIS-1 | default (A0) | 2 | 0.705 | 0.456 | 0.708 | 0.361 |
   | OASIS-1 | matched (A3) | 2 | 0.835 | 0.526 | 0.842 | 0.141 |

   **The reading:** on F, default → matched moves up *and right* (precision +0.10, recall +0.12); on
   H, precision rises the same (+0.10) but recall falls (0.24 → 0.08).
   **The caveat the caption must state:** H has 40 prior states, all at one slice level, so H recall
   measures how much of R⁻ 40 seeds can reach. A0's samples drift away from their seed (other slice
   levels), so its recall falls less. The drop is a statement about where W/8's population diversity
   comes from (the seed set), not a test of unseen against seen subjects.
   **Sensitivity (in `F3.md`, not plotted):** the H recall against R5 (`H_r5`), with one sentence.
3. **Panel (b) quantities.** All are shares of the reference's between-image variance
   $\sum_i P_{\text{ref},i}$ (non-DC), per configuration:
   - **ideal within-seed share** $1-I$, the linear-Gaussian prediction for a sampler that
     regenerates exactly the variance the prior removed (`results_discussion.md` §2a.6;
     `inherited_band_constants.json`). It depends only on the terminal blur: IXI 0.9966 (W/2) and
     0.9205 (W/8); OASIS-1 0.9983 and 0.9574;
   - **measured within-seed share** $1-I_w$, with
     $I_w = 1-\tfrac{50}{49}D_{pix}(W^2-1)/\sum_i P_{\text{ref},i}$ (D23; `inherited.within_seed_share`).
     Per run in table 1c (`docs/RESULTS/tables/tables.json`). It is measured on the **H** set (40
     unseen seeds × 50). Seed means: IXI A0 0.342, A2 0.347, A1 0.262, A3 0.262; OASIS-1 A0 0.448,
     A3 0.323;
   - **ρ** $=(1-I_w)/(1-I)$, the regenerated share (`exploratory.regeneration_ratio`; per run as
     `regen_ratio` in `docs/RESULTS/exploratory/exploratory.json`). Seed means: IXI A0 0.343,
     A2 0.348, A1 0.285, A3 0.285; OASIS-1 A0 0.449, A3 0.337;
   - **total variance ratio** of the 2,000 training-seeded samples, $\sum P_S/\sum P_{\text{ref}}$
     (`10**log_vr` in `exploratory.json` `per_run`). Seed means (±0.005): IXI A0 0.665, A2 0.671,
     A1 0.848, A3 0.885; OASIS-1 A0 0.663, A3 0.818 (`results_discussion.md` §8). It is measured on **F**, not on H. Verify that its
     denominator is the same non-DC reference power as $I_w$'s; if it is not, say so in `F3.md` and
     in the caption.

   **The reading:** every model regenerates only 28–45% of the variance its prior removes
   (ρ 0.28–0.45; ideal 1). Going to W/8 lowers the ideal within-seed share by 8% (0.997 → 0.920)
   but the measured one by 23% (0.342 → 0.262). The total spread nevertheless rises (0.66 → 0.89),
   because at W/8 most of the population variance comes from the 2,000 distinct seeds.
4. **Statistics.** Small markers are runs, large markers seed means; print n per configuration in the
   legend or caption (IXI 3/2/2/3, OASIS-1 2/2). No p-values and no "significant". Precision, recall
   and ρ are exploratory or post hoc; say so in the caption.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┐

└───────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Colours:** the arm colours of `style.ARMS` (default blue, +prior green, +spacing amber, matched
  orange). **Datasets** by marker shape (IXI circles, OASIS-1 squares), never by colour. **Sets** by
  fill (F filled, H hollow).
- **Arrows** default → matched, one for F and one for H (IXI). Decide whether OASIS-1 gets arrows or
  only mean markers; legibility first.
- **Panel (b):** IXI's four configurations, then OASIS-1's two, separated by a gap and labelled.
  The ideal share is an outline bar, the measured share a filled bar of the arm colour with run dots,
  the total variance ratio a marker (e.g. a diamond) on the same axis; a dotted line at 1 = real.
- The smallest font is 7 pt (house sans); mathtext for $1-I$, $1-I_w$, ρ.
