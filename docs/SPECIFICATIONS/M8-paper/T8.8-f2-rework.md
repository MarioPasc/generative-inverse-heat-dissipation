## Purpose and decisions

On 2026-10-07 Mario decided that the paper will have **one** results figure instead of F2 + F3. F2
(`f2_arms.*`) is reworked; F3 stays in the repository but leaves the paper. His decisions, among the
options main offered:

1. **Panel (b) becomes "Fidelity vs Diversity": precision against recall.** It replaces "realism vs
   copying". The x-axis there was copying, which is memorisation, not diversity, and copying and
   recall move in opposite directions. This is the F3 panel (a) content:
   - training seeds F, filled; unseen seeds H, hollow; against R⁻;
   - IXI's four configurations, with default → matched arrows for F and H;
   - OASIS-1 as seed means with grey arrows.
2. **A memorisation plot beside it**, in panel (b) because it is the same topic, with no new letter.
   It is a **distance-to-seed plot**, not a PCA. A 2-D PCA of brain slices is dominated by slice
   height and brightness, so it cannot show a nearest-neighbour property such as copying.
3. **Panel (c), the spectrum**, gets **band-pass images**, one per octave, as wide as the space
   between ticks. Each shows only that octave's modes of one IXI slice, i.e. what structure lives in
   each band. They are not cumulative images: F1(a) and FM already show the image forming.
4. **Layout and style:**
   - the "configurations" legend is removed;
   - (c) runs full width;
   - more space between (a) and the lower panels;
   - the y-axis of (c) goes down to −1, with the images inside the empty lower band;
   - the "kept by matched prior" legend moves to the bottom;
   - "head outline" and "ventricles, WM ring" are adjusted or removed, because the band images now
     carry that information;
   - Title Case for titles, headers and axis labels.
5. **Not done:** a per-slice-height prior. It is future work only, a sentence in the paper.

## Scientific content that must be exact

### Panel (b), left: Fidelity vs Diversity

- **Numbers:** exactly those of `F3.md` / `paper_f3`. Import the loaders from `ihdm.analysis.paper_f3`
  and assert the same expectation tables, e.g.:
  - IXI default F 0.655/0.343, H 0.661/0.2375;
  - matched F 0.757/0.4625, H 0.7585/0.0825;
  - OASIS-1 matched F 0.834/0.525.
- **The axes:** precision (Inception, k = 5, against R⁻) and recall against R⁻.
- **The caption** keeps the H caveat:
  - 40 prior states at one slice level;
  - against the slice-matched reference R5, H recall still falls, 0.277 → 0.171.

### Panel (b), right: copying of the seed, not of the training set (post hoc)

- **Distances** are pixel L2 distances with the DC removed, exactly as in `ihdm.metrics.memorisation`.
  - Reuse its nearest-neighbour code and its reference: R⁻ against the full training split.
  - $D_{\text{ref}}$ = the median distance from an R⁻ image to its nearest training image; it is
    M's denominator.
  - All quantities are divided by $D_{\text{ref}}$, so **1 = as far as a new real image is from the
    training set**.
- **Per sample:**
  - **F**, training-seeded, 2,000 per run:
    - $q_{\text{seed}}$ = d(sample, its own seed image) / $D_{\text{ref}}$;
    - $q_{\text{train}}$ = d(sample, nearest training image of *another* subject) / $D_{\text{ref}}$.
  - **H**, unseen-seeded, 40 × 50 per run:
    - $q_{\text{seed}}$ = d(sample, its own unseen seed) / $D_{\text{ref}}$;
    - $q_{\text{train}}$ = d(sample, nearest training image) / $D_{\text{ref}}$.
- **Runs:** all IXI runs, i.e. default 3, +spacing 2, +prior 2, matched 3. Show per-run medians as
  small markers and the seed mean as a large marker; add a grey band for the interquartile range of
  real R⁻ images' $q_{\text{train}}$. OASIS-1 is optional; report it in `F2.md` either way.
- **The form is your choice**, e.g. dot-and-whisker per configuration with four columns (F seed,
  F train, H seed, H train). It must answer, at a glance:
  - does $W/8$ bring samples close to their own seed for both training and unseen seeds
    (inheritance)?
  - are unseen-seeded samples any closer to the training set than a new real image ($q \approx 1$)?
- **Anchor check, asserted by the build:**
  - recompute M = median over F of d(sample, nearest training image incl. own seed) / $D_{\text{ref}}$
    for `ixi_A0_s1` and `ixi_A3_s1`;
  - it must equal `M` in `_results/index.csv` within 0.005;
  - the seed-NN fraction must match within 0.005.
- **Do not pre-judge the result.** Report the medians in `F2.md`, and write the caption from what the
  numbers show. Label the plot post hoc.

### Panel (c): Spectrum, with band-pass images

- **The curves:** unchanged (`lsd_octaves`, seed means and run dots), as in the current F2.
- **The images:**
  - the same IXI slice as F1 and FM (dataset index 3705, `reference_example(root, "ixi",
    np.random.default_rng(2026))`);
  - for each octave [c_lo, c_hi) (the last one closed at 96), the inverse orthonormal DCT of that
    slice's coefficients restricted to the octave's modes, DC excluded. Use `paper_fm.octave_masks`;
  - shown with a symmetric grey map, each image scaled to its own maximum |value|. Say so in the
    caption ("each band scaled to its own range");
  - each image is as wide as its octave on the log axis, so the 64–96 one is narrower or cropped. It
    sits in the empty band between y = −0.55 and −1, and never overlaps a data point.
- **The shading** "kept by the matched prior" ($\exp(-(2\pi c\sigma/W)^2)$ at σ = 24 px) must not hide
  the images. Draw it above the image strip only, or behind the images with the images on top;
  decide, and log the choice.
