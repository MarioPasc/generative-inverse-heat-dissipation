# T4.2 — Memorisation ratio $M$, within-seed diversity, PCA around the seed

Milestone M4 · mode: parallel with T4.1 · model: opus5-xhigh · depends on: T2.2 · unblocks: T4.3

## Mission

Implement `ihdm/metrics/memorisation.py` and `ihdm/metrics/diversity.py` per `05-metrics.md` §3,
§4 and §6, chunked so that 2000 × 3200 pixel-space nearest neighbours at $192^2$ fit in memory on
a 12 GB GPU or in CPU RAM, with tests on synthetic data and on the pilot samples.

## Ownership

`ihdm/metrics/memorisation.py`, `ihdm/metrics/diversity.py`, `ihdm/metrics/lowpass.py`
(`dct_lowpass(images, sigma_b)` using the released `DCTBlur` semantics reimplemented in numpy),
`tests/metrics/test_memorisation.py`, `tests/metrics/test_diversity.py`. Use
`ihdm.metrics.io.write_json` from T4.1 by contract (`write_json(path, obj)`); do not create your
own.

## Specification

1. `nn_distances(queries: np.ndarray[Q,H,W], corpus: np.ndarray[C,H,W], chunk=256, device) ->
   (dist[Q], argmin[Q])`: DC removed per image, `torch.cdist` in chunks, float32.
2. `memorisation_ratio(samples[Y,H,W], train[C,H,W], heldout[R,H,W], train_subjects: list[str],
   sample_seed_idx: np.ndarray[Y] | None, sigma_lp=16.0) -> MemResult(M, M_lp, d_samples_median,
   d_heldout_median, seed_nn_fraction, per_sample: dict)` per `05` §4: `samples` are
   **training-seeded** (one sample per training seed; `sample_seed_idx` gives each sample's seed
   index into `train`), the corpus is the full training split including the own seed, the
   held-out set is `ref` minus the seed subjects; `seed_nn_fraction` = fraction of samples whose
   nearest training image is its own seed (MRI: same subject as the seed, via `train_subjects`).
3. `within_seed_diversity(samples[S,M,H,W], sigma_lp=16.0) -> DivResult(D_pix_mean, D_lp_mean,
   per_seed_pix[S], per_seed_lp[S])` per `05` §3.
4. `pca_around_seed(train[C,H,W], seeds[S,H,W], samples[S,M,H,W], n_components=2, rng_seed=0) ->
   PcaResult(components, train_scores, seed_scores[S,2], sample_scores[S,M,2])` using
   `sklearn`-free randomised SVD (`torch.pca_lowrank` or `scipy.sparse.linalg.svds`) on the
   DC-removed training split (float32, 3200 × 36864 = 470 MB; fine).
5. Tests: `M ≈ 1` when the "samples" are held-out images themselves (within 5%);
   `M → 0` when the samples are exact training copies; `seed_nn_fraction = 1` when every sample
   is its own seed and 0 when every sample is another subject's image; `inherited_band` is T4.1's,
   but your `dct_lowpass` is what both use; diversity 0 for identical samples and equal to the known variance of a Gaussian
   perturbation within 5%; `dct_lowpass` at $\sigma_B$ equals the released `DCTBlur` on a random
   image to `1e-5` (`integration`-free: instantiate `DCTBlur` on CPU); PCA scores reproducible.
   `integration`: pilot samples when present.
