# T4.1 — Spectral metrics: LSD, octave profile, $T_\tau$, inherited band

Milestone M4 · mode: parallel with T4.2 · model: opus5-xhigh · depends on: T1.3 (`ihdm/spectral/power.py`), T2.2 · unblocks: T4.3

## Mission

Implement `ihdm/metrics/spectral.py` per `05-metrics.md` §1, §2 and §5 as pure functions over
numpy arrays, with tests on synthetic fields and on the pilot samples.

## Ownership

`ihdm/metrics/__init__.py`, `ihdm/metrics/errors.py`, `ihdm/metrics/io.py` (`write_json`,
`read_json`; shared with T4.2 by contract: `write_json(path, obj)` sorted keys, 6-digit floats),
`ihdm/metrics/spectral.py`, `tests/metrics/test_spectral.py`. T4.2 owns `memorisation.py`,
`diversity.py` and their tests; it will import `ihdm.metrics.io` from your branch after merge, so
keep `io.py` to those two functions.

## Specification

1. `radial_log_profile(images_float: np.ndarray[N,H,W], n_bins=48) -> (centres, log10_profile)`
   using `ihdm.spectral.power.mode_power` and log-spaced bins between 0.5 and 96 cycles per
   image; `lsd(samples, reference) -> LsdResult(lsd: float, octaves: dict[str, float],
   variance_ratio: float, n_samples: int)`; `t_tau(lsd_by_step: dict[int, float],
   threshold: float) -> int | None`; `inherited_band(samples_per_seed: np.ndarray[S,M,H,W],
   seeds: np.ndarray[S,H,W], reference_power: np.ndarray[H,W], sigma_max: float) ->
   InheritedResult(radial_measured, radial_predicted, share_measured, share_predicted)`
   per `05` §5 with `eigenvalues` from `ihdm.spectral.power`.
2. All functions accept `uint8` or float input (convert once), remove the DC per image, raise
   `MetricError` on shape mismatch, NaN, or `N < 2`.
3. Tests: `lsd(X, X) == 0`; `lsd` grows monotonically along a blur ladder ($\sigma_B \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$
   applied to half of a synthetic $1/f^2$ stack); the octave dict has the 8 keys and its RMS
   is within 10% of the 48-bin LSD on smooth spectra; `t_tau` on a hand-made dict; `inherited_band`
   on a synthetic linear model (samples = seed low band + fresh high band) gives
   `share_measured ≈ share_predicted` within 5%; `integration`: on the pilot samples of
   `$IHDM_RUN_ROOT/pilot_samples` when present.

## Acceptance

2. The LSD of the pilot's 4 × 50 samples against the `ixi` ref split is reported in the log (a number, not a judgement).
3. Log at `docs/AGENT-LOGS/M4-metrics/T4.1-spectral-metrics.md`.
