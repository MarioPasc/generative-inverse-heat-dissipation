# T3.2 — Picasso probe: three epochs on `lsun_church` and `ixi`, resume, artefacts, batch decision

Milestone M3 · mode: single · model: opus5-high · depends on: T3.1 · unblocks: T3.3

## Mission

Submit one A100 job that trains A0 for three epochs on `lsun_church` and on `ixi`, measures
throughput and peak memory at batch 32 and 64, tests resume, runs the offline sampler on the
last checkpoint, and verifies every artefact of `04-run-artifacts.md` §3. Its output fixes
`training.batch_size` (D4′) and the wall-time request of the array.

## Ownership

`slurm/probe/**` (launcher + worker following the `picasso-sbatch` skill conventions),
`docs/RESULTS/picasso_probe.md`, and the one-line commit to `configs/spectral/arms.py` that sets
`training.batch_size` (after `main` confirms).

## Specification

1. Three epochs = `ceil(3200 / batch) × 3` iterations (600 at batch 16, 400 at batch 24). The
   worker runs, in order, with `IHDM_DATA_ROOT` and `IHDM_RUN_ROOT` set:
   1. `lsun_church,A0` seed 1, batch 16, `n_iters=600, ckpt_every=200, grid_every=200, eval_every=200, log_every=20`;
   2. `ixi,A0` seed 1, batch 16, same cadences;
   3. memory probe: `ixi,A0` seed 1 at batch 24 for 40 iterations (`--config.training.batch_size=24
      --config.eval.batch_size=24`) and, only if that peaks below 30 GB, at batch 32 for 40
      iterations; record peak GB and img/s for each (D4″ decision: 16 unless 24 fits under 30 GB);
   4. resume test: re-run (ii) with `n_iters=800` on the same workdir → must resume at 600 and
      write `ema_iter_000800.pt`;
   5. `python -m ihdm.cli.sample_ckpt --run <ixi run> --ckpt ema_iter_000200.pt --source seed
      --n-seeds 4 --n-per-seed 5 --out <run>/probe_samples`;
   6. `python -m ihdm.cli.sample_ckpt ... --source train --n-seeds 64 --n-per-seed 1` (times the
      fidelity sampling per image).
   Resources: `--constraint=a100 --gres=gpu:1 --cpus-per-task=8 --mem=48G --time=02:00:00`, QOS
   `short`; logs `~/execs/ihdm/logs/probe_%j.out`. `sbatch --test-only` before submitting; record
   the job id with the skill's id-capture rule.
2. Batch decision (D4″): 16 unless batch 24 peaks at ≤ 30 GB, in which case 24 and
   `n_iters=27500`; at 16, `n_iters=40000`. Both values are written into `arms.py` together.
   The decided value is set **inside `configs/spectral/arms.py`** (both `training.batch_size` and
   `eval.batch_size`); a CLI `--config.training.batch_size` override does not move
   `eval.batch_size` (ml_collections applies flags after the factory), so probe steps that use a
   CLI override must pass `--config.eval.batch_size` as well.
3. `docs/RESULTS/picasso_probe.md`: per run: it/s, img/s, peak GB, loss at the last line,
   eval loss, the artefact listing (`ls -R` trimmed), the resume line, sampler wall time per
   sample (seconds per 200-step chain per image at batch 64); the derived numbers: A100 hours per
   20k-iteration run at the chosen batch, hours for 2k samples per checkpoint, the array's
   `--time` (1.5 × the estimate, rounded up to the hour) and the total A100-hours for 30 runs.
4. Optional fast path: the `test-picasso-loginexa` skill (V100, no queue, 30 min) for steps
   (i)–(ii) while the A100 job waits; the A100 numbers are the ones that count.

## Acceptance

1. The probe job completed; all six steps' outputs exist; the artefact listing matches `04` §3.
2. `picasso_probe.md` has the throughput/memory tables and the derived estimates; batch size
   decided and committed to `arms.py` with `main`'s confirmation.
3. Log at `docs/AGENT-LOGS/M3-picasso/T3.2-picasso-probe.md` (job id, queue wait, wall time).
