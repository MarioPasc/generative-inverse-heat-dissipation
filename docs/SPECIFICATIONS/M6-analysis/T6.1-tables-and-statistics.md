# T6.1 — Tables and statistics for the report

Milestone M6 · mode: ‖ with T6.2 (disjoint files) · model: opus55-xhigh · depends on: T5.2 ·
input: a `results/` folder written by `python -m ihdm.cli.collect_results`, nothing else

## Mission

`python -m ihdm.cli.analyse --results <results_dir> --out docs/RESULTS/tables/` turns the collected
results into the tables the report cites. Each table is written in Markdown and LaTeX (`booktabs`),
together with one machine-readable `tables.json`, and `docs/RESULTS/tables/README.md` states what
each table shows and the statistical method behind it. The procedures are the pre-registered
ones of `05-metrics.md` §8 and decisions D13, D16, D17 and D20–D22. The command is written and
tested now on the 24-run partial collection and on synthetic results, and rerun by `main` on the
final 30-run collection.

## Tables (definitions: `05-metrics.md`; the claim: `00-overview.md` §1)

1. **Cell table.** One row per run (dataset, arm, seed) with every scalar endpoint: final LSD,
   $T_\tau$, KID (+CI), FID (+CI, $N_{ref}$), recall and coverage, $M$ and $M_{lp}$, $D_{pix}$ and
   $D_{lp}$, the measured and predicted inherited band, and `n_skipped`. Use
   `ihdm.stats.tables.cell_table` where it fits.
2. **Paired contrasts against A0** per dataset: A3−A0, A1−A0, A2−A0, and A2′−A0 on Churches only.
   Each contrast is paired by seed, with a bootstrap CI (`ihdm.stats.bootstrap`) and an exact
   permutation p. Report `p_min` whenever the seed count caps it; the floor is 0.1 with 3 vs 3.
3. **The interaction**, the headline:
   $\Delta_{\text{MRI}} - \Delta_{\text{photo}}$ for A3 vs A0 (IXI vs Churches), per endpoint, with
   a CI and permutation p.
4. **Decomposition.** The share of the A3 effect carried by A1 (terminal blur alone) and by A2
   (spacing alone), per dataset, with the caveat that A1/A2 have 2 seeds.
5. **The A2′ control** (Churches-matched spacing on Churches).
6. **The transfer sign table**: the sign of A3−A0 per endpoint on IXI vs OASIS-1 and on Churches
   vs Bedrooms (2 seeds per transfer cell; report signs and magnitudes, no p-values).
7. **$T_\tau$**, as defined in `05-metrics.md` §2, computed from each run's 12-step LSD curve
   (5k…60k, `lsd_005000…lsd_060000` in `index.csv`) and the same-dataset A0 final LSD. This is not
   computed inside `evaluate_run` (T5.1/T3.5 notes).

## Honesty requirements (all go in the tables' README, in plain words)

- Training length 60k: first 40k, then extended by the pre-registered gate (D22). The 55k/60k gate
  found no further gain. Report both gates' numbers from `results/gates/`.
- Evaluation sampling in fp16 (D20); its measured effect on LSD is 5.1e-5, 0.1 % of the IXI noise
  floor.
- Churches is undertrained: final LSD ≈ 1.4, oscillating across checkpoints, blurry samples. Its
  contrasts are reported but interpreted with that caveat.
- Run 11 (`lsun_church_A3_s3`) was trained in 3 attempts; the final one resumed from its 30,001
  rolling state (D21). It carries 6 skipped steps. `lsun_church_A3_s1` shows a right-edge stripe
  artefact in its samples (T6.2 shows it).
- The FID bootstrap interval is biased upward (T4.3); KID is the headline.

## Ownership

## Acceptance

1. Tests on a synthetic 30-run results tree cover every table. Built from the T5.2 test
   builders, a planted effect must be recovered with the right sign and a CI that excludes 0.
   A null effect must give a CI containing 0. The p floor is checked.
2. On the 24-run partial collection (`$IHDM_DATA_ROOT/_results_partial_24/`) the CLI runs and
   writes every table. Tables needing a missing run (tier-3 cells 24–29) are marked
   "incomplete: n/N runs", never silently dropped.
3. The full suite is green and `ruff` is clean; the log is complete.
