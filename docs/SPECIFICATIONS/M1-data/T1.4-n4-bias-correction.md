# T1.4 — N4 bias-field correction of the registered MRI volumes; rebuild; refit; sensitivity rows

Milestone M1 (reopened 2026-09-22 evening) · mode: single · model: opus5-xhigh · depends on: T1.1, T1.3 · unblocks: T3.2 (final data on Picasso)

## Why (decision D15)

T1.3 measured that 84% of IXI's coarse-bin (0.5–1 c/img) between-image variance sits in the single
DCT mode (1, 0): an anterior–posterior intensity ramp that differs between subjects, i.e. a
receive-coil / multi-site bias-field residue (IXI: three sites, two field strengths), not anatomy.
The [Experiment-Reviewer] session recommended N4 correction on the MRI side only, on the ground
"preprocess correctly" (N4 targets an acquisition artefact of MRI, like RAS reorientation and rigid
registration already applied; photographs get nothing), **with the condition that both rows are
reported**: the claim is stated on the N4-corrected data, the uncorrected numbers are printed as a
sensitivity row, and the crossover table is recomputed after the refit, not assumed.

## Mission

Add an N4 step to `ihdm/preprocess/mri.py` applied to each **registered** volume (from the
`_cache/registered/<cohort>/` cache) before the foreground-p99 intensity scaling; rebuild `ixi` and
`oasis1`; keep the uncorrected datasets as sensitivity copies; carry the IXI site label; re-run
`build_schedules` and `profile_data` so the frozen arrays and `data_profile.md` reflect the
corrected training splits, with the uncorrected numbers alongside and a per-site coarse-share
table before and after N4.

## Specification

1. **N4** (`bias.py`): `n4_correct(volume: sitk.Image, mask: sitk.Image, cfg: N4Config) -> sitk.Image`
   with SimpleITK `N4BiasFieldCorrectionImageFilter`, **default parameters** (default spline
   distance / control points, default iterations `[50, 50, 50, 50]`, convergence threshold
   0.001), mask = the template brain mask dilated 10 mm (the same foreground mask the p99 scaling
   uses; use `image > 0.5` or an explicit upper bound of 1 in `BinaryThreshold`, never a uint8
   overflow — see T1.1's log §6), shrink factor 4 for the field estimate then the field applied at
   full resolution (`GetLogBiasFieldAsImage` on the full-resolution input), output clipped at 0.
   **Do not tune any N4 parameter against the coarse share.** Cache the corrected volume as
   `_cache/registered_n4/<cohort>/<subject>.nii.gz` with a sidecar recording the parameters and
   the log-field range; skip cached subjects unless `--force`.
2. **Site label**: `index.csv` gains no column (frozen contract); the site is recorded in
   `meta.json.parameters.sites` as `{subject: site}` for IXI (`Guys`, `HH`, `IOP` from the
   filename) and `{subject: "WashU"}` for OASIS-1, and in each registered sidecar.
3. **Rebuild**: same 400 subjects, same splits (the subject selection and `split_by_subject`
   are seeded; assert the subject lists are identical to the `_sensitivity` copies), same slices;
   `meta.json.parameters.n4 = {...}` and `pipeline_version = "1.1"`.
4. **Refit** (`build_schedules`): all seven arrays; `schedules.json` updated with the new hashes.
   `arms.py` must still build all 30 cells.
5. **Profile** (`profile_data`): every table gets a `no N4` sensitivity column (from the
   `_sensitivity` copies) next to the N4 column; the checklist is evaluated on the N4 data with
   the uncorrected outcome printed beside it; new table: coarse share (0.5–1 c/img) and the
   share of the (1,0) mode within it **per IXI site**, before and after N4; the crossover table is
   recomputed; `fig_data` regenerated.
6. **Tests**: N4 on a synthetic phantom with a multiplicative linear ramp recovers a flat
   intensity within 3% (integration, template needed); the site parsing; the sensitivity-copy
   move is idempotent.
