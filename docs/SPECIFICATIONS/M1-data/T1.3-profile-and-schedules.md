Port the spectral functions of the TFM analysis scripts into `ihdm/spectral/`, compute the profile
of each dataset's **training split** (3200 images), reproduce the known results, build and freeze
the schedule arrays of `04-run-artifacts.md` §1, and produce the report's data figure.

(per-dataset npz of the profiles), `tests/spectral/**`.

## Specification

1. **`ihdm/spectral/power.py`**: `mode_power(images: np.ndarray[N,H,W] float) -> np.ndarray[H,W]`
   (mean-centred across images, orthonormal DCT-II, per-mode variance; DC set to 0),
   `radial_profile(power, bins)`, `octave_bins()` (the 8 bins of `05-metrics.md`),
   `octave_shares(power) -> dict[str, float]`, `fit_alpha(power) -> float` (least squares of
   $\log P$ on $\log n$ over $1 \le n/2 \le 48$ cycles per image, the same range as the analysis
   scripts; say which in the log), `eigenvalues(n_pix)`, `inherited_share(power, sigma_max)` =
   $\sum P e^{-2\lambda t}/\sum P$ with $t = \sigma_{\max}^2/2$.
2. **`ihdm/spectral/schedules.py`**: `ScheduleSpec(kind: "log"|"matched", sigma_min=0.5, sigma_max,
   K=200, fitted_on: str | None)`; `log_schedule(spec)`; `matched_schedule(power, spec)` running
   the constant-arc-length march between $t(\sigma_{\min})$ and $t(\sigma_{\max})$ for **the
   spec's own endpoint** (D12); `levels_per_octave(schedule)`; `per_level_spread(power, schedule)`
   (max/min of the between-image variance removed per step, the "spread" of the tables);
   `save_schedule(path, schedule, meta)` / `validate_schedule(path)` per `04-run-artifacts.md` §1;
   `ScheduleError`.
3. **`build_schedules` CLI**: builds `log_W2`, `log_W8`, `ixi_W2`, `ixi_W8`, `lsun_church_W2`
   (arms) and `oasis1_W8`, `lsun_bedroom_W2` (tables only) from the training splits, writes the
   `.npy` files and `schedules/schedules.json` with hashes and the `levels_per_octave` table.
4. **`profile_data` CLI**: for each dataset's training split (and, as a check, its ref split):
   $\alpha$, octave shares, inherited shares at $W/8$, $W/4$, $W/2$, the log-schedule spread, the
   IXI-matched spread, the own-matched spread; and the crossover table (each dataset under
   log / IXI-matched / own). Writes `docs/RESULTS/data_profile.md` with tables in the layout of
   learning/03 §16.1 and §16.3, a column with the archived unregistered numbers for comparison,
   and a "known results" checklist: (i) MRI $\alpha$ exceeds photograph $\alpha$ by ≥ 0.5;
   (ii) the 0.5–1 c/img share is below 3% on both MRI sets and above 15% on both photograph sets;
   (iii) the log-schedule spread is larger on MRI than on photographs; (iv) the IXI schedule
   reduces OASIS-1's spread and increases Churches'; (v) the two brain schedules agree within
   35% (max) and the photograph schedule differs from IXI's by more than 50%. Each item PASS/FAIL
   with the numbers.
5. **Figure** `fig_data`: one example per dataset (MRI: slice index 5 of one *ref* subject chosen
   with rng 2026; photographs: one ref image), and the radial between-image variance share per
   mode on the training splits, log–log, $\alpha$ in the legend. **Author it at 2.7 × 4.35 in
   (aspect 1.61), with the colours and rcParams of
   `projects/GenAI/analysis/proposal_data_figure.py`** (Paul Tol blue/cyan for MRI, red/orange
   for photographs; the figure is printed at ≈ 2.1 in wide in a wrapfigure, so fonts must stay
   legible at that scale); PDF + PNG at 300 dpi, matplotlib only. Print the four $\alpha$ values
   and the image count on stdout and in `data_profile.md`: the proposal's caption (currently
   "192 images", "$\alpha \approx 2.3$–$2.6$ / $3.6$–$3.7$") is updated from them by the
   [Proposal-Specifier] session, not by this ticket.
6. **Tests**: on a synthetic $1/f^\alpha$ Gaussian field ($\alpha = 2$, 512 images at $64^2$)
   `fit_alpha` recovers $2.0 \pm 0.15$; `matched_schedule` on a white spectrum has equal
   per-level removed variance within 1%; `log_schedule` equals the released formula; schedule
   endpoints and monotonicity; `levels_per_octave` sums to $K$; an `integration` test that, when
   the TFM analysis path exists, the ported `mode_power` and `matched_schedule` agree with the
   originals on a random field to `rtol=1e-6`.
