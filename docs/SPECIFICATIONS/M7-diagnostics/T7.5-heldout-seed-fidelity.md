## Question

Every fidelity number of the experiment (tables 1a, 2 and 3) is computed on *training-seeded*
samples, whose prior state is a blurred training image (the paper's Alg. 2). At W/8 that prior hands
over the training image's coarse layout: 56–76% of samples have their own seed as nearest training
image.

**Does A3's fidelity gain over A0 depend on the seed being a training image, or does it hold when
the prior state comes from a subject the model never saw?** On IXI the gain is precision +0.088 and
KID −63%.

**What the check can and cannot separate.**

- Held-out seeds close the *memorisation* route: the prior cannot hand over a training image.
- They do not close inheritance. At W/8 the prior still hands over the coarse layout of a real, if
  unseen, brain.
- The check therefore separates "the gain is tied to the training set" from "the gain generalises
  to unseen prior states".
- It cannot show "better generation from scratch", which W/8 by construction never does.

## Protocol (pre-registered on 2026-10-03, before any number of this ticket exists)

1. **Reference R⁻.** The dataset's `ref` split minus every image of the 40 seed subjects.
   - Expected size: 40 subjects × 10 slices = 400 images on MRI; 800 − 40 = 760 on photographs.
   - It is shared by every arm and by both sample sets of a dataset.
2. **Sensitivity reference R5 (MRI only).** The slice-5 images of the 320 `train` subjects: 320
   images, the population at the held-out seeds' slice level.
   - Held-out-seeded samples cannot inherit these images through the prior.
   - R⁻ is conservative for A3: A0's samples drift across slice levels, which an all-slice
     reference does not penalise.
   - R5 is liberal for A3.
   - A conclusion that holds under both references is robust.
3. **Features.**
   - Inception pool features from `ihdm.metrics.inception.inception_features(images_u8,
     device="cpu", mode="clean")`.
   - Computed for every sample set, for R⁻ and R5, and for the full `ref` (needed for the anchor).
   - All features are recomputed on this machine with one extractor; none is mixed with the
     GPU-computed caches.
4. **Metrics, per run and per sample set.** H is the held-out-seeded set and F the training-seeded
   set.

   | role | metric |
   |---|---|
   | **primary** | precision (k = 5) against R⁻, for H and for F |
   | **secondary** | density against R⁻; KID against R⁻ (`kid_from_features`, production defaults); on MRI, the precision and KID of H against R5 |
   | **descriptive** | recall and coverage, which are capped because H has only 40 distinct prior states |

   **Within-run 95% CI** for precision and density: a percentile bootstrap of the per-sample
   indicators (precision) or counts (density), 1,000 draws, rng 0.
   - For H, resample the 40 seeds as whole clusters of 50 samples. For F, resample samples.
   - The per-sample values must reproduce the aggregate of `recall_coverage` to 1e-12; use
     `prdc`'s own distance helpers.
5. **Contrasts.** For each dataset and run seed $s$:

   $$\Delta_H(s) = m_H(\text{A3}, s) - m_H(\text{A0}, s), \qquad \Delta_F(s) = m_F(\text{A3}, s) - m_F(\text{A0}, s),$$

   and the same with A1 in place of A3.
   - Statistics: `ihdm.stats.paired_delta` and `ihdm.stats.permutation_test`, used exactly as in
     tables 2a and 2b (p_min is 0.1 at 3 seeds and 1/3 at 2).
   - Also report the generalisation gap $m_F - m_H$ of every run.
6. **The reading (IXI; primary endpoint: precision against R⁻), applied literally.** Let $G_F$ and
   $G_H$ be the means of $\Delta_F(s)$ and $\Delta_H(s)$ over the 3 run seeds. Apply the first
   rule that matches:
   1. $G_F \le 0$, or not all three $\Delta_F(s) > 0$: *"the comparator shows no precision gain on
      R⁻; not evaluable on precision"*. Then apply the rules below to KID, with the gain defined
      as A0 − A3, and label the result secondary.
   2. All three $\Delta_H(s) > 0$ and $G_H \ge 0.5\,G_F$: **"the fidelity gain generalises to
      unseen seeds"**.
   3. All three $\Delta_H(s) > 0$ and $G_H < 0.5\,G_F$: **"partly tied to training seeds"**.
   4. Otherwise: **"not shown on unseen seeds"**.

   - **OASIS-1:** the same rule with 2 run seeds, read descriptively (transfer).
   - **Churches and Bedrooms:** descriptive only, because the photograph models fail.
   - **The A0 sanity expectation.** At W/2 the prior carries 0.3% of the variance, so for A0
     $m_F - m_H$ should be near 0. Report it; do not gate on it.
7. **Reproduction anchors, run before the full run.**
   - (a) The local IXI `ref` features against `ixi/_features_inception_ref.npy`: report the
     maximum |Δ| and the minimum row-wise cosine similarity.
   - (b) For `ixi_A0_s1` and `lsun_church_A3_s1`: precision, recall, density and coverage of F
     against the FULL `ref`, from local features, must reproduce the stored `final.json` values
     within 0.01 absolute, and KID must fall inside its stored 95% interval.
   - If an anchor fails, stop and message `main`.

### Amendments (main, 2026-10-03, made before any A3 − A0 contrast was computed)

The agent raised a corner case of step 6, and main ruled by `SendMessage`. The rulings are
recorded in the agent log §2 and in the results README.

- **The KID fallback applies the whole rule, rule 1 included.**
  - If the KID comparator also fails ($G_F^{\text{KID}} \le 0$, or not all KID gains of F
    > 0), the outcome is "not evaluable on precision or KID".
  - Without this, a failing KID comparator would satisfy $G_H \ge 0.5\,G_F$ trivially.
- **The R5 sensitivity covers H only:** per-seed $\Delta_H(\text{R5})$, its mean, and sign
  agreement.
  - F against R5 is printed descriptively only, because the training seeds include the R5 images.
  - The ratio rule is not applied on R5.
