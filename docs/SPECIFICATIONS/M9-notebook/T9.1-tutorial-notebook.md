## 4. Notebook content

Title: **"Matching the prior and the level spacing of IHDM to brain MRI: a tutorial"**. Runs on CPU in under one minute, reading only files tracked in the repository (no data drive, no GPU, no checkpoint).

0. **Introduction** (one short paragraph plus a bullet list "In this tutorial you will…"). What IHDM is in two sentences; the two settings we change (terminal blur of the prior, spacing of the blur levels) and why (the paper chose them for natural images); what the notebook does not do (train or sample; results are loaded). Links: the IHDM paper (arXiv 2206.13397) and `docs/RESULTS/results_discussion.md`.
1. **Setup.** Imports from `ihdm` and the released `model_code`; the repository-relative paths; a check that the assets exist.
2. **Two kinds of images.** The two IXI slices and the two Churches images side by side (§5 gives the rows and splits; label each image with its split), one sentence on each dataset (registered T1 brain slices, 192×192; LSUN Churches photographs as preprocessed for this project — take the preprocessing from the brief).
3. **The forward process: heat instead of noise.** Course notation: the VP/DDPM marginal $q(\mathbf{x}_t\mid\mathbf{x}_0)=\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\mathbf{x}_0,(1-\bar\alpha_t)\mathbf{I})$ shrinks every direction by the same factor; IHDM replaces $\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\mathbf{I}$ by $F(t)=\mathbf{V}e^{-\boldsymbol\Lambda t}\mathbf{V}^\top$, $\lambda_i=\pi^2(n_i^2+m_i^2)/W^2$, $\sigma_B=\sqrt{2t}$, and keeps a small fixed noise: $q(\mathbf{u}_k\mid\mathbf{u}_0)=\mathcal{N}(F(t_k)\mathbf{u}_0,\sigma^2\mathbf{I})$. Code: implement $F(t)$ in a few lines with `scipy.fft.dctn(norm="ortho")`, then show that it equals the released PyTorch operator (`model_code.utils.DCTBlur`) on one slice (print the maximum absolute difference). Figure: one IXI slice and one Churches image at $\sigma_B\in\{0, 2, 8, 24, 96\}$ px. One sentence on $\lambda_i t=(c_i\sigma_B/\omega)^2$, $\omega=W/(\sqrt2\pi)\approx43.2$: fine detail goes first.
4. **Where the between-image variance lives.** Define $P_i$ (report Eq. 5). Load `power_train` from `docs/RESULTS/data_profile/{ixi,lsun_church}.npz`. Figures: (a) radial spectrum, log–log, IXI and Churches, with a $c^{-2}$ reference line; (b) octave shares (eight octaves 0.5–1 … 64–96 c/img) for IXI, Churches, and a synthetic $P(c)\propto c^{-2}$ built on the same 192² grid with `ihdm.spectral.power.power_law(192, 2.0, 1.0)`. The printed shares must match `data_profile.md` §2. **Expert finding, verified by main:** on the discrete grid the synthetic $c^{-2}$ shares are 23.7/15.2/12.2/11.2/10.7/10.5/10.4/6.1%, not equal: the 0.5–1 octave holds only three modes and 64–96 c/img is half an octave, so equal shares (the continuum statement of the report, Appendix C) hold only roughly from 4 c/img up. Show this and say it in one or two sentences; never claim exactly equal shares on the grid.
5. **Change 1: the prior.** The prior $\hat p_K$ (report Eq. 4) and the seed. Figure: the noise-free prior state $F(t_K)\mathbf{x}$ of one IXI slice and one Churches image at $W/2=96$ and $W/8=24$ px. Then the inherited share $I(\sigma_{B,K})=\sum_i d_{K,i}^2P_i/\sum_iP_i$ (report Eq. 6) as a curve against $\sigma_{B,K}$ for IXI and Churches, with markers at 24 and 96 px. The printed values must match `data_profile.md` §3 (training split): IXI 8.5% and 0.3%, Churches 29.1% and 1.8%. Say that $I$ is a prediction for an ideal sampler, and that its cost (copying) is measured in §7.
6. **Change 2: the level spacing.** Define $E_k$ (report Eq. 7) and say what it is: the between-image variance of the level-$k$ regression target. Load the four frozen schedules (`log_W2`, `ixi_W2`, `log_W8`, `ixi_W8`) from `schedules/`. Figures: (a) $\sigma_{B,k}$ against $k$ (log scale) for the four; (b) levels per $\sigma_B$ octave (must match `data_profile.md` §5); (c) $E_k$ against $k$ on IXI for log versus matched at both terminal blurs, with the spread $\max E_k/\min E_k$ printed (log_W2 8.9×, ixi_W2 ≈1.00×; give the W/8 values the code computes); (d) $E_k$ under `log_W2` for the synthetic $c^{-2}$ spectrum next to IXI and Churches. Verified spreads (main): synthetic $c^{-2}$ 2.0×, Churches 3.8×, IXI 8.9×; at $W/8$, `log_W8` gives 1.5× / 3.5× / 8.5× and `ixi_W8` gives 1.0× on IXI. The message: in the continuum limit log spacing gives a flat $E_k$ for $c^{-2}$ data (report Eq. 8, Appendix C); on the 192² grid the coarse octaves hold few modes, so even $c^{-2}$ data give 2.0×; photographs are close to that floor, IXI is far from it. Do not call the synthetic curve flat. Mention, without running it, how the matched spacing is fitted (Algorithm 1 of the report; `ihdm.spectral.schedules.matched_schedule`) and why the notebook loads it.
7. **Results (loaded, not recomputed).** Two sentences on the experiment (four configurations A0–A3, IXI with 3/2/2/3 seeds, OASIS-1, 60,000 iterations; see the report §4.4). Re-plot from `assets/results/*.json` simplified versions of Figure 2: (b-top) precision against recall for sets F and H; (b-bottom) median $q_{\text{seed}}$ and $q_{\text{train}}$ (report Eq. 11); (c) octave errors $e_o$ (report Eq. 10). Then show the full `docs/RESULTS/paper/f2_arms.png`. The text says how to read each panel and states only what the numbers show (as in the caption draft of `F2.md`); no conclusions beyond it, and mark panel (b) as post hoc.
