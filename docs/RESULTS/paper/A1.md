> **The natural-image reference runs at our budget (default configuration, A0).** (a) Training loss against iteration: running mean over 1,000 iterations; thin lines are runs (Churches 3, Bedrooms 2), thick lines their mean. The top axis gives epochs at 200 iterations per epoch (3,200 training images, batch 16); dots mark the checkpoints of (b); shading marks the 40k → 60k extension, resumed at iteration 40,001. About 98% of each level's regression target is the added training noise and only about 2% the deblurring that forms the image, so the loss is dominated by denoising: its plateau shows that the optimisation settled, not that the samples converged. (b) One training seed per dataset (Churches image 1180, Bedrooms image 1180: the first index of the frozen 500-seed evaluation list) and its sample at 5k, 15k, 30k, 45k, 60k iterations, run seed 1, drawn with the same sampling noise at every checkpoint (common random numbers, asserted), so the columns differ only by training. ## Text (appendix A1)

> As natural-image references, we trained the configurations of Sec. 3 on LSUN Churches (all four, plus the A2′ spacing control) and Bedrooms (default and matched); Table A1 lists how this setup differs from the paper's. The training loss levels off after about 25k iterations (125 epochs; Fig. A1a), yet the samples stay blurry (Fig. A1b). Inception precision is at most 0.12 in every photograph configuration, against 0.63–0.84 on MRI.
