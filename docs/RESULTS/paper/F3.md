## Caption draft

> **Seeding from many training images raises realism and diversity together; within a seed, every model under-disperses.**
>
> (a) Inception precision and recall (k = 5) against R⁻, the reference split without the 40 seed subjects (400 images). Filled: 2,000 training-seeded samples (F); hollow: 40 unseen seeds × 50 (H). Circles: IXI (n = 3/2/2/3 for default/+spacing/+prior/matched; small: runs), squares OASIS-1 seed means (n = 2/2). Default → matched: F moves up and right (precision 0.655 → 0.757, recall 0.343 → 0.463); H gains the same precision (0.661 → 0.759) but loses recall (0.238 → 0.083). H has 40 prior states, all at one slice level, so its recall measures how much of R⁻ 40 seeds reach: W/8's population diversity comes from the seed set.
>
> (b) Shares of the real between-image variance (non-DC, full reference split, 800 images): outline, ideal 1 − I; fill, measured 1 − I_w on H (dots: runs); ρ = (1 − I_w)/(1 − I); diamond, total variance ratio of F. Models regenerate 28–45% of the removed variance. W/8 lowers 1 − I_w by 23% (0.342 → 0.262) while the total rises (0.66 → 0.89).
>
> Precision, recall and ρ are post hoc or exploratory; no significance tests.

## Denominator of the total variance ratio

**Verdict: the same.** The total variance ratio (`10**log_vr` = `final.json` `variance_ratio`) and I_w divide by the same non-DC reference power ΣP_ref.

- *Code.* `run_eval` passes `views.reference` (the full `ref` split) to `metrics.spectral.lsd`, whose `variance_ratio` is ΣP_S/ΣP_ref over every non-DC mode (`mode_power` of uint8/255 with each image's DC removed). `inherited.reference_spectrum` computes P_ref of the same `ref` split with the same `mode_power` of ref/255 and the DC mode zeroed. In the orthonormal DCT, removing an image's mean changes only the DC coefficient, so the two non-DC sums are the same number.
- *Data.* Every one of the 14 runs' `final.json` `n_reference` equals the constants' `n_ref` (IXI 800, OASIS-1 800); `inherited_predicted` of every `final.json` equals the constants' I (checked above).
- *Not the same reference as panel (a).* Precision and recall use R⁻ (400 images on IXI and OASIS-1: the `ref` split without the 40 seed subjects); the variance ratio and I_w use the full `ref` split (IXI 800, OASIS-1 800). The caption says so.
