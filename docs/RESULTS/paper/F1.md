## Caption draft (177 words)

**IHDM's defaults assume natural-image statistics; brain MRI departs from both.** (a) IHDM generates by restoring frequency bands, coarse to fine. Top: noise-free heat states u(σ_B) of one IXI slice, each above c = 43.2/σ_B cycles per image, the frequency of the DCT mode whose length-scale is σ_B. Bars: IXI between-image variance per octave; dashed: LSUN Churches; dotted: the equal share per octave of a 1/f² spectrum, which log spacing assumes. Shading: variance kept by the default prior (σ_B,max = 96 px; 0.3% handed over) and the matched prior (24 px; 8.5%). Rugs: the 200 reverse levels of each schedule, counted per σ_B octave. (b) The mean of 3,200 training slices, and the noise-free priors of three held-out subjects. (c) The four configurations: default (A0), +prior (A1), +spacing (A2) and matched (A3). One unseen subject, sampled from the same seed image and noise in both arms. Over three seeds, matched lowers KID by 63%, while the share of training-seeded samples whose nearest training image is their own seed rises from 6% to 72%.
