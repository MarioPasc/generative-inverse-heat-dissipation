## Caption draft

> **Matching the prior to brain anatomy drives fidelity; matching the spacing adds a small spectral correction.**
>
> (a) Samples of the four configurations from the same seed images and the same sampling noise (IXI, run seed 1, 60k iterations; two training seeds, images 3311 and 2488, and one unseen subject, image 5, chosen by a pre-declared rule; no seed was skipped). $u(\sigma)$ is the noise-free prior state at the default ($\sigma$ = 96 px, W/2) and the matched ($\sigma$ = 24 px, W/8) terminal blur; sampling starts from it plus noise.
>
> (b) Per-sample realism (Inception precision, k = 5, against the reference split without the seed subjects, 400 images) against copying (the share of samples whose nearest training image is their own seed). Small markers are runs (n = 3/2/2/3) and large markers are means. From default to matched, copying rises from 6% to 72% and precision from 0.655 to 0.757. The left strip gives realism on unseen subjects, where copying is impossible: 0.661 to 0.759, 96% of the gain on training seeds. +spacing changes precision by +0.025 on training seeds and +0.033 on unseen subjects (paired, seeds 1–2). Grey arrow: the OASIS-1 cohort, default to matched (0.705 to 0.834; copying 2% to 56%).
>
> (c) Per-octave spectral error of 2,000 samples against real images ($\log_{10}$ power ratio; 0 = real). default lacks power at 1–2 and 2–4 c/img (-0.30 and -0.39: 50% and 41% of the real power); +spacing reduces the deficit to -0.24 and -0.34; with the matched prior it is gone (|·| ≤ 0.05). Every configuration stays below 0 above 4 c/img. The shading is the share of each frequency's variance that the matched prior hands over, $\exp(-(2\pi c\sigma/W)^2)$ at $\sigma$ = 24 px.
>
> Precision is exploratory; the pre-registered KID and LSD are in Table 1.
