### Panel (c): the band-pass images

- **Slice:** IXI dataset image 3705, `reference_example(root, "ixi", np.random.default_rng(2026))` (the slice of F1 and FM; asserted).
- **Bands:** for each octave [c, 2c) (the last closed at 96 c/img), the inverse orthonormal DCT of the slice's coefficients restricted to that octave's modes (`paper_fm.dct_weights`, `paper_fm.octave_masks(192)`, `paper_fm.synthesise`); the DC and the corner modes above 96 c/img belong to no band. Bands + DC + corner = slice (tested to 1e-10).
- **Crop:** every band shows the same square window, rows 20–191 and columns 9–180 (172 px; the smallest square centred on the pixels above 0.1 of the slice), so the head fills the image. Each image is square, as tall as the strip between −1 and −0.55, centred in its octave; the 64–96 octave is narrower than the strip, so its image shows the centre columns of the window at the same pixel scale.
- **Grey map:** symmetric, mid-grey = 0, each band scaled to its own range (± its largest |value| in the window; slice on [0, 1]): 0.5–1: 0.146, 1–2: 0.344, 2–4: 0.222, 4–8: 0.407, 8–16: 0.537, 16–32: 0.357, 32–64: 0.390, 64–96: 0.191.
- **Shading:** the share of variance kept by the matched prior is drawn above the image strip only (from −0.55 up, as a fraction of the space to the top of the axes), so it never covers an image.
