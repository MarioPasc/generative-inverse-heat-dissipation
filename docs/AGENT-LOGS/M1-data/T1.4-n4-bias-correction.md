**Decisions taken during the work** (each with the alternative rejected and why):

- **D-T1.4-1 — `n4_correct` returns an `N4Result` (corrected image, log bias field, diagnostics),
  not the bare `sitk.Image` the ticket's signature sketch names.** The sidecar has to record the
  log-field range inside the mask and the QC sheet has to draw the field, so both callers need the
  field that the filter already produced. Returning only the corrected image and recomputing the
  field in the caller was rejected: it would either run the estimate twice or force the caller to
  reach into the filter object.
- **D-T1.4-2 — `N4Config` carries the SimpleITK defaults explicitly and a unit test pins them
  against `N4BiasFieldCorrectionImageFilter()`'s own getters.** Not setting anything at all is the
  most literal reading of "default parameters", but then `meta.json.parameters.n4` could not list
  "every parameter actually used" without querying the filter, and a future SimpleITK whose defaults
  moved would silently change the data. Setting the documented defaults and asserting they equal the
  library's defaults gives both properties. Measured on SimpleITK 2.5.6: iterations `(50,50,50,50)`,
  convergence threshold `0.001`, spline order 3, control points `(4,4,4)`, 200 histogram bins,
  Wiener noise `0.01`, bias-field FWHM `0.15`, mask label 1.
- **D-T1.4-3 — N4 runs on the 400 selected subjects only, not on the whole registered cache.**
  The gate and the seeded draw happen before the slicing and are unaffected by N4 (they read the
  registration sidecars), so correcting the 181 IXI and 16 OASIS-1 volumes that never enter the
  dataset would cost wall time and 4 GB of cache for nothing. Rejected: correcting the full cohort
  "in case the gate moves" — the gate is frozen and the cache is keyed by subject, so a later change
  simply corrects the new subjects on demand.
- **D-T1.4-4 — the site label is written into the N4 sidecar and into
  `meta.json.parameters.sites`; the *registration* sidecar gains it only for subjects registered
  from now on.** The ticket asks for it "in each registered sidecar", but the registered cache is a
  T1.1 artefact and rewriting 997 sidecars would mean re-registering (7 h) or editing files this
  ticket does not own. Rejected: touching the cached sidecars in place — it would desynchronise them
  from the volumes they describe and from T1.1's log. The site is recoverable from `index.csv`'s
  `source` column for every dataset, corrected or not, which is what the per-site table uses.
- **D-T1.4-5 — the per-site table is computed from `index.csv`'s `source` column, not from
  `meta.json.parameters.sites`.** The sensitivity copies were written by pipeline 1.0 and have no
  `sites` key; deriving the site from the raw filename works identically on both, so the before/after
  columns are produced by one code path. Rejected: back-filling `sites` into the sensitivity copies'
  `meta.json` — it would modify datasets the ticket says to keep untouched.
- **D-T1.4-6 — the "uncorrected" checklist is evaluated with schedules refitted on the uncorrected
  training splits**, not with the N4-fitted frozen arrays. Items (iii)–(v) are statements about a
  dataset *and its own matched schedule*; scoring the uncorrected data against N4-fitted schedules
  would measure the correction, not the uncorrected pipeline, and would not reproduce T1.3's
  published outcome. The refit of `ixi_no_n4_W2` and `oasis1_no_n4_W2` costs seconds, and it was
  verified to reproduce T1.3's frozen arrays exactly (§4), which is what makes the `no N4` column
  the published pipeline rather than an approximation of it.
- **D-T1.4-7 — in the two tables whose columns are already the octaves (§2 octave shares and §5
  levels per $\sigma_B$ octave) the "no N4" sensitivity is an extra *row*, not an extra column.**
  The ticket says "column" everywhere; those two tables have no free column axis, and the archived
  pre-registration figures are already rows there, so rows keep one convention. Rejected: a second
  table repeating the same eight octaves — it would double the page for no extra information.
- **D-T1.4-8 — the before/after QC sheet scales each image panel by its *own* foreground p99 and
  divides the field panel by its median inside the mask.** N4's log field carries an arbitrary
  additive constant, so a shared display scale makes a corrected slice look uniformly brighter for
  a reason the pipeline removes one line later. Panels scaled the way the intensity rule scales
  them show what actually enters `images.npy`. Rejected: one shared scale (the first version,
  looked at and discarded because the global gain dominated the comparison).

**Assumptions proceeded on:**

- The dilated template brain mask is used as N4's mask exactly as the ticket freezes it, including
  the voxels where the registered volume is exactly zero because they fall outside the acquisition's
  left-right field of view. ITK sets `log(x) = 0` for non-positive `x` inside the mask rather than
  failing, so this is safe; the fraction of masked voxels that are exactly zero is recorded per
  subject in the N4 sidecar (`mask_zero_fraction`). **Resolved by measurement:** the median is
  0.75 % on IXI and 1.32 % on OASIS-1, the maximum 7.0 % on either cohort (§5.1), so the effect is
  negligible and no mask change was needed. Had it been large, the fix would have been to intersect
  the mask with the acquisition field of view, which is a contract change.
- The ticket's N4 acceptance test ("a synthetic phantom with a multiplicative linear ramp ...
  uses the real template mask if present") is read as *a synthetic phantom on the template grid
  with the real dilated mask*, not as *the MNI152 T1w image itself with a ramp imposed on it*.
  MEASURED, and the reason the reading matters: on the template image itself, N4 with defaults
  removes only about 73 % of an imposed 0.8 → 1.2 ramp (34.3 % residual profile spread → 9.2 %),
  because the template is a non-linear average with tissue peaks the histogram sharpening does not
  separate the way it separates a real acquisition's. The synthetic phantom on the real grid and
  mask passes the 3 % criterion. Both readings are in the test file; the failing one is not
  asserted, and the number is reported here because it is a real limit of N4-with-defaults on
  low-frequency fields and it is consistent with §5.2.

### 5.3 The per-site table, which explains why

| dataset | site | subjects | coarse share, N4 | coarse share, no N4 | $(1,0)$ share, N4 | $(1,0)$ share, no N4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IXI | Guys | 159 (train) | **3.11 %** | 3.51 % | **89 %** | 89 % |
| IXI | HH | 120 (train) | **2.90 %** | 4.12 % | **70 %** | 76 % |
| IXI | IOP | 41 (train) | **2.62 %** | 3.21 % | **73 %** | 78 % |
| OASIS-1 | WashU | 320 (train) | **2.02 %** | 2.44 % | **86 %** | 88 % |

Two facts follow, and they are the reason the table was asked for.

1. **Within every site N4 does reduce the coarse share** (Guys 3.51 → 3.11 %, HH 4.12 → 2.90 %,
   IOP 3.21 → 2.62 %), and it reduces it most at HH, the 3 T site, where the receive-field
   inhomogeneity is largest. The stage works.
2. **The pooled IXI coarse share (4.07 %) is larger than every individual site's** (2.62–3.11 %).
   The excess is a between-site component, and it survives N4: correcting each volume's own
   smooth field cannot remove a systematic difference in the *mean* anterior–posterior profile
   between two scanners, because after correction each volume is normalised by its own
   foreground p99 (a scalar), not matched to a common intensity template.

So the $(1,0)$ ramp is **not** purely a bias field, and it is **not** purely a multi-site
artefact either: it is still 70–89 % of the coarse bin *inside a single site*. Part of it is
plausibly anatomy on these ten axial planes (the anterior–posterior asymmetry of the head inside
a 192-px window) and part of it is a between-scanner intensity difference.

## 6. Open questions, risks, follow-ups

- **N4 did not remove IXI's $(1,0)$ ramp, so D15's stated rationale is only half-supported** —
  severity: high for how the report words the claim — owner: `main` and the report author. The
  ticket said to apply N4 and report both rows, and that is done; but the sentence "the coarse
  bin is a coil/multi-site residue rather than anatomy" is **not** what the data says after
  correction. §5.3 has the evidence. The defensible wording is: the coarse bin is small on MRI
  either way (2–4 % against 23–24 %), it is dominated by one anterior–posterior mode, and a
  standard per-volume bias-field correction does not remove it, so at least part of it is not a
  bias field.
- **Item (ii) still fails on IXI, now at 4.07 % instead of 3.96 %** — severity: medium — owner:
  `main`. Nothing was retuned to move it. The threshold is `< 3 %` on *both* MRI sets; OASIS-1
  is at 2.02 %. If the orchestrator wants the item to read on the design's actual requirement
  (MRI coarse share far below the photographs') the threshold is the thing to revisit, not the
  data; that is a decision for `main`, not for this ticket.
- **A between-scanner intensity-template normalisation would be the next step if the ramp
  matters** — severity: low — owner: `main`. Piecewise-linear histogram matching (Nyúl & Udupa,
  IEEE TMI 19(2):143–150, 2000) after N4 is the standard follow-up and would address the
  between-site component that N4 structurally cannot. It is out of scope here and it would be a
  second contract change, so it was not done.
- **`_cache/registered_n4/` adds about 16 GB to `$IHDM_DATA_ROOT/_cache`** — severity: low —
  owner: `main`. It is a cache, not a dataset, and is not synced to Picasso.
- **The registered sidecars of the 997 cached volumes still carry no `site` field** (decision
  D-T1.4-4). Anything that needs the site reads `index.csv`'s `source` column or
  `meta.json.parameters.sites`; only a subject registered from now on gets it in the
  registration sidecar as well.

## 7. Deliberately not done

- Any N4 parameter search. Every value is SimpleITK's default, pinned by
  `test_n4config_matches_simpleitk_defaults`, and the only knob this ticket chose is the shrink
  factor the ticket itself froze at 4.
- Re-registration. The registered cache is T1.1's and was reused; the gate reproduced T1.1's
  numbers exactly (IXI median −0.1353 MAD 0.0211, 539 passing; OASIS-1 −0.1201 / 0.0152, 405),
  which is the check that nothing moved underneath.
- Any change to the photograph datasets, the trainer, the sampler or `configs/`.
- Intensity-template normalisation across sites (see §6).
- Picasso syncing of the rebuilt `ixi`/`oasis1`, which the ticket assigns to the orchestrator.
- `docs/HARNESSES/data.md` §4 still quotes the pre-N4 expectations; it is not in this ticket's
  ownership list, so it was left for `main`.
