**Decisions taken during the work** (each with the alternative rejected and why):
- **One job, not two.** The six steps projected to ≈60–95 min against `short`'s 2 h cap, so they
  fit in one `--time=01:50:00` submission. Splitting into two jobs was rejected because it doubles
  the queue wait (measured at ≈66 min for this job) for no measurement gain.
- **A deadline guard on the two 128-chain sampler steps instead of a shorter sampler run.** After
  step 5a the worker knows the real s/chain and projects `128 × s/chain` for 5b and 5c; if that no
  longer fits before the 1h50 wall minus 7 min, the step is *skipped and reported as skipped*.
  Trimming the chain count to make it fit was rejected: a sampler timing measured on fewer chains
  than the budget assumes is not the number T3.3 needs, and a silently shortened measurement is
  worse than a missing one.
- **`--config.training.grid_every=1000000` on the batch-24 memory probe.** The grid sampler runs
  at batch 8 under `no_grad` and would dilute the training-memory peak the step exists to measure.
  `train.py` still forces one grid at `step == n_iters`, which is accepted.
- **Every step wrapped in a status-recording `run_step` (no `set -e`).** The batch-24 OOM is the
  expected outcome of step 3; aborting the worker there would lose steps 4–6.
- **Kept `IHDM_RUN_ROOT` on FSCRATCH rather than `$LOCALSCRATCH`.** The ticket names the path, the
  probe writes tens of large files rather than thousands of small ones, and the run directories
  have to survive the job for the artefact listing. The `$LOCALSCRATCH` question belongs to T3.3,
  which is where the file-count pressure is (see §6).

**Assumptions proceeded on** (messaged to `main` where they affect another ticket):
- The pre-N4 cluster copies of `ixi` are adequate for a throughput/memory probe: N4 changes
  intensity statistics, not tensor shapes, so it cannot move it/s or peak GB. If wrong, only the
  loss values in §5 are affected, not the budget.
- The sampler was run without `--amp`, matching T2.2's 3060 measurement, so the two are comparable.
  An AMP sampler would be faster and is not measured here.

## 6. Open questions, risks, follow-ups

  does not choose. The reduced plan saves 508 A100-h (58 % of the campaign) and costs: $T_\tau$
  resolution drops from 2,500 to 10,000 iterations, intermediate LSD uses 500 instead of 1,000
  samples, and FID/KID move from 5k to 2k samples with correspondingly wider bootstrap CIs.

  task. `sacctmgr` confirms `medium_uma` is available to this account. Severity: low, but the T3.3
  worker must not inherit `--qos=short` from this probe's script.

  wall-time choice.

  window is a stale 33.28 GiB; the authoritative figures are in the OOM message itself. If a future
