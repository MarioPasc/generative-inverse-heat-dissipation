## 2. Plan and decisions

**Restatement.** Build F3 (5.5 in wide, 2.0–2.4 in tall), a two-panel paper figure. Panel (a) plots
precision against recall (Inception, k = 5, against R⁻) for F (2,000 training seeds, filled markers)
and H (40 unseen seeds × 50, hollow markers), with default → matched arrows. Panel (b) shows within-seed
diversity: the ideal 1 − I, the measured 1 − I_w with its runs, ρ, and the total variance ratio, with
a dotted line at 1. Every number is read from the result files, cross-checked and asserted against
the ticket's tables. The outputs must be byte-stable, editable in Inkscape, and documented in
`F3.md`.

**Plan:**
1. Read the ticket, the framing claims, `paper_f2.py` (its public helpers), `inherited.py`,
   `exploratory.py`, `style.py`, `run_eval`/`metrics.spectral` (the denominator), the held-out
   README and `results_discussion.md` §2a.6/§4.1/§8.
2. Prototype every number against the ticket's tables before writing code.
3. Write `paper_f3.py` (readers, cross-checks, expectation checks, drawing, layout audit, notes) and
   the CLI.
4. Iterate on the layout by rendering the PNG and looking at it.
5. Write the tests, build the committed outputs twice, run the fast suite, commit, write the log.

**Decisions taken during the work** (each with the alternative rejected and why):
- **OASIS-1 in panel (a): seed-mean squares with grey arrows, no run markers** (`RUNS_IN_A = ("ixi",)`).
  The alternative, full OASIS-1 run markers, was drawn in iteration 2 and rejected. Its eight extra
  light squares landed among the IXI F runs in the 0.35–0.6 recall band, so the two cohorts could
  not be told apart at 5.5 in. The OASIS-1 per-run values are in the `F3.md` table, and the caption
  says "squares OASIS-1 seed means".
- **Panel widths about 57/43.** Panel (a) axes are 2.22 in and panel (b) axes 1.62 in, plus about
  0.65 in of direct labels on the right. The alternative, 60/40, left the ρ row (six 2-decimal
  numbers) touching at 7 pt. 55/45 with a separate legend would not fit the "measured 1 − I_w" key.
- **Panel (a) legend below the axes, not inside.** There is no free corner: the lower-left holds the
  default H runs, the upper-left the matched H means, and the lower-right a +prior F run. The legend
  is drawn as a two-row key of separate artists with ids. Fill (F/H) is on row 1; shape (IXI/OASIS-1)
  and run/seed-mean size are on row 2.
- **ρ as a row of numbers above the bars**, with a "ρ" row label on the left, at y = 1.06 above the
  dotted line at 1. The alternative, a secondary row under the axis, would have competed with the
  35° rotated configuration labels and the IXI/OASIS-1 group labels.
- **Panel (b) bars overlaid, not side by side.** The outline bar (1 − I) and the filled bar
  (1 − I_w) share one slot, so the fill fraction of each outline *is* ρ. Side by side would need 12
  slots at about 0.13 in each.
- **Panel (b) key as direct labels on the last group** ("ideal 1 − I", "total (F)", "measured /
  1 − I_w (H)") with leader lines, in place of a legend row: there was no width for a legend row
  under 1.62 in.
- **Total variance ratio: seed-mean diamond plus a run-range line behind it.** Small run diamonds
  were tried in iteration 1 and rejected. At three runs within 0.03 of each other they read as a
  grey shadow of the large diamond.
- **Run dots of 1 − I_w use the dataset shape** (circles IXI, squares OASIS-1). Colour is never the
  dataset, as the frozen contract requires. The total-variance diamond is the one non-dataset shape,
  named in the key.
- **Arrows coloured by cohort, not configuration:** INK for IXI and `#8a8780` for OASIS-1, the
  OASIS-1 grey of F2. An arrow joins two configurations, so neither arm colour fits.
- **Expectations:** OASIS-1 matched F is corrected and bound cases use 4 decimals (see §6).
  Main accepted both on 2026-10-07.
- **Errors.** `PaperF3Error(AnalysisError)` with `F3MissingInputError`, `F3ExpectationError` and
  `F3ConsistencyError`. Calls into `paper_f2` (`read_index`, `runs_of`, `merge_heldout`) and
  `inherited.load_constants` are wrapped, so a perturbed duplicate raises the F3 error class. The
  alternative, subclassing `PaperF2Error`, would have made F2's errors indistinguishable from F3's.
- **Reuse.** `merge_heldout`, `read_index`, `runs_of`, `paper_style`, `save_paper_figure`,
  `audit_layout`, `file_sha256`, `IXI_ARMS`/`OASIS_ARMS`/`ARM_LABELS`/`DATASET_NAMES`/`PAPER_WIDTH_IN`
  are imported. `within_seed_share` and `load_constants` come from `inherited`, and
  `regeneration_ratio` from `exploratory`. `paper_f2._check` is private, so I did not import it; F3
  has its own short `_check` with the same inclusive bound (main: OK). The SVG hashsalt is set to
  `ihdm-t8.5` by a nested `rc_context` inside `paper_style()`.
- **Layout audit extended.** `audit_layout` gives the size and the smallest font. F3 adds a height
  window of 2.0–2.4 in and a text-overlap check: rendered boxes of drawn texts must not intersect.
  Tick labels outside the view or on axes switched off are excluded. Rotated texts (the 35° panel (b)
  tick labels) are excluded because their axis-aligned boxes overlap by construction; they were
  checked by eye at 500 dpi.
- **Caption numbers are rounded half up** (`_r`): 0.2375 → 0.238, as in the ticket's own table.
  Python's `:.3f` gave 0.237 for A0 H recall (binary float 0.23749…), and the caption must not
  contradict the ticket.
