**What the numbers show (for Mario's hypothesis; the figure lets the reader check it):**
- With the W/8 prior, samples sit much closer to their own seed than with W/2, and as much for
  unseen seeds as for training seeds (IXI H 1.28 → 0.87, F 1.29 → 0.89): inheritance, seen or
  unseen.
- On IXI, unseen-seeded W/8 samples are *not* closer to the training set than default samples
  (H train 0.90 → 0.93; F train, other subjects, 0.91 → 0.94). H train sits just below the R⁻
  band for every IXI configuration (0.898–0.927 against 0.936–1.078), default included, i.e. all
  samples are a little closer to the training set than a new real image regardless of the prior;
  F train of the W/8 arms (0.94) is just inside the band. This matches M < 1 at W/2 (0.90–0.92),
  which `results_discussion.md` §2a.5 attributes to L2 favouring smoother images.
- On OASIS-1 the direction of H train differs: 0.99 → 0.93 (both inside OASIS-1's band,
  0.89–1.09). So "not closer to the training set" holds on IXI but not as a general statement;
  F2.md reports both.
