### Decisions taken during the work

Three of them are corrections to the frozen contract as restated in the prompt. They are listed
first because the orchestrator must see them; each is stated with its derivation.

**Status: all three were messaged to `main` while the work was in progress and `main` approved all
three**, confirming the derivation ("y - x has variance (1-d)^2 P + (1-d^2) P = 2(1-d) P, so the
residual about the prior state d_K x_s is the quantity whose variance is (1-d^2) P") and stating
that it is amending `05-metrics.md` §2 (43 populated bins at W = 192) and §5 (residual about
$d_K x_s$; both shares over all non-DC modes, the low-band variant reported beside) on `main`.
The code in this branch is therefore the approved reading, and the amended spec is `main`'s to
land.

- **D-T4.1-1 (contract defect, corrected) — the inherited-band residual is taken about the
  *prior state* $d_K \hat x_s$, not about the raw seed $\hat x_s$.** The prompt freezes
  $V(i,j) = \operatorname{mean}_{s,m}(\hat y_{sm} - \hat x_s)^2$ *and* the prediction line
  $1 - d_K^2 = 1 - e^{-2\lambda_n t_K}$. The two are inconsistent. Under the linear-Gaussian
  model the ticket itself prescribes for the test — $\hat y_{sm} = d\,\hat x_s + \varepsilon_{sm}$
  with $\operatorname{Var}\varepsilon = (1-d^2)P$ — the literal residual gives

  $$\mathbb E(\hat y - \hat x)^2 = (1-d)^2 P + (1-d^2)P = 2(1-d)P,$$

  so `radial_measured` would equal $2(1-d)$ against a prediction of $1-d^2=(1-d)(1+d)$: a ratio
  $2/(1+d)$, i.e. exact agreement only as $d \to 1$, 33% high at $d = 0.5$ and 100% high as
  $d \to 0$. The share is worse: $1 - \sum V/\sum P = 2\sum dP/\sum P - 1$, which is **negative**
  whenever the $P$-weighted mean of $d$ is below $1/2$. Measured on the real `ref` spectra, the
  literal formula returns $-0.976$ for IXI and $-0.873$ for LSUN Churches at
  $\sigma_{B,\max} = 96$ (and $-0.765$ / $-0.260$ at $\sigma_{B,\max} = 24$) against predictions
  of $0.0034$ / $0.0179$ and $0.0795$ / $0.2886$: the frozen formula cannot produce the frozen
  quantity, on any dataset, at either terminal blur. Implemented instead

  $$V(i,j) = \operatorname{mean}_{s,m}\left(\hat y_{sm}(i,j) - d(i,j)\,\hat x_s(i,j)\right)^2,
  \qquad d(i,j) = e^{-\lambda_{ij}\sigma_{B,\max}^2/2},$$

  which is exactly $(1-d^2)P$ under the model, makes `radial_measured` and `share_measured`
  estimate the prediction with no bias, and uses all three of the arguments the frozen signature
  carries (`seeds` **and** `sigma_max`; under the literal formula `sigma_max` would only enter the
  prediction). $d(i,j)$ is the released `DCTBlur` kernel at $\sigma_{B,\max}$ — the same
  $\exp(-\lambda t)$, $t = \sigma^2/2$ — so $d\hat x_s$ *is* the seed blurred to level $K$, i.e.
  the prior state the chain starts from, minus the prior noise. §5's own justification ("the
  surviving part $d_K x_{\text{seed}}$ is constant across the 50 samples and contributes no
  variance") is the sentence this implements. Alternatives rejected: (i) the literal residual —
  produces a curve that cannot match its own prediction line and a negative share; (ii) the
  within-seed variance about the sample mean $\operatorname{mean}_m(\hat y_{sm}-\bar{\hat y}_s)^2$
  — also unbiased for $(1-d^2)P$ and model-free, but it discards the systematic part of the
  deviation (a model whose samples sit on the wrong prior state would look perfect), and it leaves
  the `seeds` argument unused.
- **D-T4.1-2 (contract defect, corrected) — the LSD is the RMS over the *populated* log bins, 43
  of the 48 at $W = 192$, not over all 48.** With 48 log-spaced bins between 0.5 and 96 cycles per
  image and $n = \sqrt{i^2+j^2}$ integral-radius modes, five bins hold no mode at all (indices
  1, 2, 4, 5, 8; the DCT radii jump 1, $\sqrt2$, 2, $\sqrt5$, … while the bins step by
  $192^{1/48} = 1.1157$). `ihdm.spectral.power.radial_profile` returns `nan` there by contract, so
  a literal 48-bin RMS is `nan` for every stack and would make `write_json`'s `allow_nan=False`
  fail. The mask is a property of the grid alone (`W` and `n_bins`), identical for the sample and
  the reference stack, so the metric stays a fixed, deterministic functional; it also lets the
  same function run on the 32² and 64² test fixtures, where the bins above the Nyquist frequency
  are empty as well. Bins where either profile is non-positive are dropped too (a blurred stack
  underflows to zero power at high frequency) and the count is reported. Rejected: interpolating
  across the empty bins (invents data), or changing the bin edges (breaks the frozen 0.5–96/48
  grid).
- **D-T4.1-3 (contract ambiguity, resolved) — `share_measured` is taken over *all* non-DC modes,
  the same set `inherited_share` uses, and the low-band restriction is exposed as an optional
  keyword and reported as a sensitivity line instead.** The prompt asks for `share_predicted =
  inherited_share(P_ref, sigma_max)` (all non-DC modes) and `share_measured = 1 - \sum V/\sum P`
  *restricted to the low band*. Those two are not comparable: under the linear model
  $\text{measured} = \sum_B P d^2/\sum_B P$ and $\text{predicted} = \sum_{\text{all}} P d^2/\sum_{\text{all}} P$,
  and since $d^2 \approx 0$ outside the low band the restriction multiplies the measured share by
  $\sum_{\text{all}} P/\sum_B P$ — measured on the `ref` spectra, the low band at 8 px holds
  31.9% of IXI's non-DC variance and 66.4% of Churches', i.e. an inflation of **3.13×** and
  **1.51×** — so the headline pair would disagree by construction, by a factor that depends on the
  dataset, and the H-METRICS 5% identity could not hold. With both sums over all
  non-DC modes the identity is exact. The restriction is still available as
  `inherited_band(..., low_band_sigma_px=8.0)`, which masks **both** sums (the prediction by
  passing the masked `P_ref` to the frozen `inherited_share`, i.e. reuse, not duplication).
  **The exact rule, as required by the prompt:** a mode $(i,j)$ is in the low band when its
  characteristic blur scale $\sigma_n = \sqrt{2/\lambda_n} = \sqrt2\,W/(\pi n)$ is **at least**
  `low_band_sigma_px`; equivalently $n \le \sqrt2\,W/(\pi\,\sigma_{\text{px}})$, which at
  $W = 192$, $\sigma_{\text{px}} = 8$ gives $n \le 10.80$, i.e. **≤ 5.40 cycles per image**
  (the modes still standing after a blur of $\sigma_B = 8$ px). The prompt's phrase "modes with
  sigma-space octave ≤ 8 px" read literally ($\sigma_n \le 8$) selects the *fine* modes, which
  cannot be "the low band"; the complementary reading is the one implemented. Both numbers are in
  §5 and in `docs/RESULTS/metrics_bracket.md`.
- **D-T4.1-4 — `write_json` rounds to 6 *significant* digits, not 6 decimal places.** `05` §9 says
  "floats rounded to 6 digits". `round(x, 6)` silently zeroes every quantity below $5\times10^{-7}$
  — permutation $p$-values (T4.3 draws 10,000 permutations, so $p$ can be $10^{-4}$ and an exact
  enumeration can be smaller), bootstrap interval widths, and the inherited share at
  $\sigma_{B,\max} = 96$ on a steep spectrum. `float(f"{x:.6g}")` agrees with 6 decimal places for
  every quantity of order 1 (all the LSDs, shares and ratios) and keeps the small ones. Rejected:
  `round(x, 6)` — data loss at no benefit.
- **D-T4.1-5 — floating-point input is not down-cast to `float32`.** The contract says "convert
  once to float32 in [0,1]". Integer input is divided by 255 into `float32` exactly as asked
  (this is the real path: `samples.npy` and `images.npy` are both `uint8`). Floating input is left
  in its own dtype, because `mode_power` accumulates in `float64` anyway and a
  `float64 → float32 → float64` round trip on a synthetic test field only loses digits. No range
  check is imposed: the synthetic fields of the tests are zero-mean, the DC is removed by every
  function, and a range check would reject them for no scientific reason.
- **D-T4.1-6 — `variance_ratio` sums over all non-DC modes** (including the 7 728 of 36 864 modes
  above 96 cycles per image), consistent with `inherited_share`, which the ported module documents
  as "every non-DC mode of the grid contributes, including those above 96 cycles per image". The
  96 c/img cut is a property of the *binned* quantities (§1 states it for the octave bins), and
  `variance_ratio` is explicitly the **total** variance ratio. The choice is close to immaterial:
  the modes above 96 c/img are 21.0% of the grid but hold 0.06% of IXI's `ref` variance and 0.67%
  of Churches'. Rejected: cutting at 96 — it would make `variance_ratio` disagree with the
  inherited share's normalisation for no stated reason.
- **D-T4.1-7 — the octave profile uses `radial_profile(power, OCTAVE_EDGES)` (mean per-mode
  variance per octave), not `octave_shares`.** §2 defines the octave profile as
  $\log_{10}\bar P_S(b) - \log_{10}\bar P_R(b)$ with $\bar P$ the radial profile of §1, i.e. a bin
  *mean*, while `octave_shares` returns normalised shares. Using shares would subtract the total
  variance ratio out of every octave and the H-METRICS identity (octave RMS within 10% of the
  48-bin LSD) would fail on any pair with a variance offset. `octave_shares` is therefore not
  used here.
- **D-T4.1-8 — the per-image DC removal is done chunk-wise through a small read-only adapter**
  (`_DcRemoved`) handed to `mode_power`, instead of materialising a converted copy of the stack.
  `evaluate_run` (T4.3) calls `lsd` with the 2 000-sample final set and, at 192², an eager
  `float32` copy is 295 MB (737 MB for the 5 000-sample set of an earlier budget); the adapter
  keeps the peak at one chunk. It also lets a `np.memmap` of `images.npy` be passed straight in.
  Three call sites use it (`radial_log_profile`, `lsd`, `inherited_band`'s reference path), which
  satisfies the "no abstraction without two concrete users" rule of `02` §1. Removing the per-image
  DC is, in exact arithmetic, a no-op for every non-DC mode (subtracting a constant changes only
  the (0,0) coefficient), so this is a contract formality, not a change of the numbers.
- **D-T4.1-9 — `t_tau` raises on an empty dict.** "Never reached" is `None`; an empty
  `lsd_by_step` means the caller found no checkpoints, which is a bug in the caller, and `02` §3
  asks for fail-fast validators. Non-finite LSD values and a non-finite threshold also raise.
- **D-T4.1-10 — `radial_measured` and `radial_predicted` are both *unweighted* bin means of a
  per-mode quantity** ($V/P_{\text{ref}}$ and $1-e^{-2\lambda t}$ respectively), as the prompt
  writes them, so the two curves are weighted identically and the per-bin 10% identity is
  apples-to-apples. The share, by its definition, is the $P_{\text{ref}}$-weighted version of the
  same quantity; the two therefore differ in weighting by construction, which is stated in the
  results section. Rejected: making `radial_measured` the ratio of bin means
  $\bar V(b)/\bar P(b)$ (variance-weighted, consistent with the share) — it would need a
  variance-weighted prediction too, which is not what the contract freezes.

## 6. Open questions, risks, follow-ups

- **`InheritedResult.radial_measured` and `radial_predicted` hold `nan` in the bins that hold no
  mode** (five of the 48 at $W = 192$). `write_json` refuses non-finite floats by contract
  (`allow_nan=False`), so T4.3 must either drop those bins before writing the curves or write only
  the two shares (which is what `05` §9 names: `inherited_measured`, `inherited_predicted`). Low
  severity, but it will raise `MetricError` the first time it is hit. Owner: T4.3.
- **The MRI reference-versus-reference floor is inflated by subject clustering.** `ref[:400]` and
  `ref[400:]` are disjoint groups of 40 subjects each, so the "noise floor" of IXI and OASIS-1
  mixes estimator noise with a real between-subject difference; splitting the same 800 images
  interleaved (slices of the same subject on both sides) lowers it substantially, and both numbers
  are in `docs/RESULTS/metrics_bracket.md`. When a run's final LSD is compared against "the floor"
  in the report, say which floor. Medium severity for interpretation, none for the code. Owner:
  T6.1.
- **At $\sigma_{B,\max} = 96$ the predicted inherited share is 0.34% on IXI and 1.79% on
  Churches** (`ref` splits). A measured share of the same size is at the mercy of the model's own
  high-frequency power, since $V \approx P_{\text{model}}$ where $d \approx 0$ and the whole
  spectrum is in the denominator. The mechanism figure is informative at $\sigma_{B,\max} = 24$
  (7.95% / 28.9%) and near-degenerate at 96; the low-band variant
  (`low_band_sigma_px=8.0`) is the more legible number for the A0 arm. Owner: T6.1 / the report.
- **`t_tau` raises on an empty mapping.** If T4.3 wants "no checkpoints evaluated" to be reported
  as "not reached", it must catch `MetricError` or check the mapping first. Low severity.
