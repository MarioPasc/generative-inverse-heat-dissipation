**Decisions taken during the work** (each with the alternative rejected and why):
- **W rule (approved by `main`)**: for W ≥ 128 the fine grid is 48 log bins 0.5 → W/2 c/img
  (populated bins only), octave edges are the powers of two from 0.5 below W/2 then W/2, the
  inherited low band is 8·W/192 px and the low-pass σ of D_lp / M_lp is 16·W/192 px. At W = 192
  these are the frozen constants bit for bit (8.0·192/192 = 8.0 exactly; `logspace(…, log10(96.0))`
  identical). For W < 128 the frozen constants are kept, explicitly and only for the 96² fixture
  of `tests/metrics/test_run_eval.py` (`tests/analysis` reads its `lsd_octaves['64-96']`); this is
  documented in the module docstring and a code comment in `spectral_grid`, and pinned by
  `test_below_128_the_legacy_constants_are_kept`. **Real data are only ever 128² or 192².**
  Alternative rejected: scaling at every W (changes every number of the 96² fixture and breaks
  `tests/analysis`).
- **Where the rule applies:** `lsd`, `radial_log_profile` and `inherited_band` infer W from the
  stacks they are given; `log_bin_edges` / `log_bin_centres` keep the frozen 0.5–96 grid unless
  called with the new keyword `n_pix`, because `ihdm.stats.bootstrap` and `ihdm.analysis` call
  them without an image. Alternative rejected: passing a grid only from `run_eval` (any other
  128² caller of `lsd` would then silently use bins above Nyquist and an empty `64-96` octave,
  which raises).
- **δ trees (approved):** `samples[_amp-<mode>]_delta-<repr(float(delta))>/` and the same for
  `metrics`; an explicit `--delta` always gets the tagged tree, even at the default value; the
  `delta` key is added to `request.json`, `ckpt_*.json`, `final.json` and `summary.json["sampling"]`
  only when overridden. A tagged tree that holds a set drawn at another δ is refused (never
  overwritten). Alternative rejected: untagged tree when δ equals the default (two requests that
  differ only by `--delta 0.0125` would then share or not share a cache depending on the config).
- **Budget (approved):** `--final-from-lsd` (`EvalRequest.final_from_lsd`) reuses the final
  step's 500-seed LSD set as the final set of the Inception metrics and M. `final.json` records
  `final_set: "lsd"` and the intermediate list's digest; a cached `final.json` is reused only if
  its `final_set` (absent = `"final"`) matches the request, so the default path never reads a
  record computed from the LSD set and vice versa. Per condition 500 + 40×5 = 700 chains;
  12 × 700 = 8,400 chains ≈ 5.6 A100-h at 2.42 s/chain.
- **`paired_lsd_gate` (approved):** keeps its 0.5–96 grid; the sweep does not use it; listed as a
  follow-up (§6).
- **Image size check:** `evaluate_run` raises if the run's `data.image_size` differs from the
  dataset's `meta.image_size` (`DatasetViews.image_size`, a new defaulted field).
- **Path-dependent digests in the goldens:** `config_sha256` and `checkpoint_sha256` depend on the
  temporary directory the fixture is written to (the config holds `data.root`, its digest is
  pickled into every checkpoint), so the golden drops them together with timestamps and timings;
  every metric value, key and other path-normalised field is compared.
- **`prepare_eval.py` unchanged:** it already works at 128² (proved by
  `test_prepare_eval_works_on_a_128_dataset` with the Inception extractor stubbed); nothing in it
  assumes 192. T7.3 needs rows for the new datasets in `slurm/eval/expected_seed_lists.csv`
  (not owned by T7.2; §6).
- **Sweep job:** a new worker `slurm/diag_eval/delta_sweep.sbatch` built on `eval_array.sbatch`
  and the helpers of `slurm/eval/common.sh` (shadow run on `$LOCALSCRATCH`, resume from the
  task's own tar, one tar of the whole shadow tree plus one JSON per task copied back atomically,
  EXIT/TERM traps); code at `~/execs/ihdm/wt/T7.2` (rsync, `GIT_SHA` beside it).

## 6. Open questions, risks, follow-ups

- **Gate at W ≠ 192:** `ihdm.stats.bootstrap.paired_lsd_gate` keeps the frozen 0.5–96 grid; at
  W = 128 its bins above 64 c/img are empty and its octaves would include `64-96`. Not used by

- **T7.3 needs `expected_seed_lists.csv` rows** for `lsun_church_r128` and `lsun_church_n32k`
  before `prepare_eval` can write their caches.
- **Proposed amendment of `05-metrics.md` §1, §3–§5** (for `main` to apply after the merge):

  > **Image side.** The constants of this section are defined at W = 192. For a dataset of side
  > W ≥ 128 every grid scales with W: the fine profile has 48 log-spaced bins between 0.5 and W/2
  > cycles per image (the LSD is the RMS over the populated ones: 43 at W = 192, 42 at W = 128);
  > the octave edges are the powers of two from 0.5 that lie below W/2, then W/2 (eight bins
  > ending `64-96` at 192, seven ending `32-64` at 128); the low band of the inherited share is
  > σ_n ≥ 8·W/192 px and the low-pass length-scale of D_lp and M_lp is 16·W/192 px, so both are
  > fixed in cycles per image (the low band is n ≤ 10.80, 5.40 c/img, at every W). Below 128 px
  > the W = 192 constants are kept; this case occurs only in the synthetic test fixture.
